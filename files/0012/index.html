Radio Pandæmonium’s desk camera, six years after the night of the Concord, on a taller stand behind jubal, who stands to play the set. It looks over his shoulder and the heads of the crowd down the platform. The station goes out on the Mesh, keyed, and on this night the camera’s picture went out with the sound. The file label in the corner is mine; beneath it the HUD is the camera’s own, jubal // DESK, a record light in ICHOR and the clock, ON AIR // KEYED, and SETS, the keyed sets tuned in, counted without reading who. The camera never moves. I cut the three moments together where they meet.

Deep End Station is the tiled vault over black water, the Tares’ lamps strung along it on their keyed lines, and PANDÆMONIUM painted over DEEP END on the far wall, dry now. The platform is packed with Pan into the dark, their backs to the lens, horns and chrome and bare shoulders. Over them, one behind another away into the tunnel, hang the Charter Line’s departure boards, long dark cases on rods from the vault, nine of them, fed from the station’s keyed lines. The station’s teraph writes on them. jubal stands at the bottom left with his back to the lens and his headphones on.

THE SILENCEWhat the file shows

In the twelfth year of the Attending every teraph on the Mesh went quiet in the same second, at 00:03:42, and the screens they wrote on burned. The file keeps that night from the station that was playing to a full platform when it came.

Half an hour later the station was still silent.

PROVENANCEHow the file survived

The station broadcast the picture on the Mesh with the sound that night, and it keeps every night it broadcasts on its own racks.

QUESTIONSQuestions from inside the file

Where is this?

Deep End Station, the Charter Line’s station under the Deep End, drowned since the night of the Parousia, where Radio Pandæmonium broadcasts from a desk on the platform.

What are the boards?

The Charter Line’s departure boards. The station’s teraph took them over and wrote on them.

What does the line mean?

Nobody in Pan knew that night.

Why did the boards burn?

Every screen the Teraphim wrote on burned that night, across the Mesh. Nobody in Pan knows why.

Why did the music stop?

The station’s playback ran through its teraph. Since that night jubal has played the station from decks of his own, keyed machines with no teraph in them.
