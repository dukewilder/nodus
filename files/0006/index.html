THE WILDERNESSWhat the file shows

An angel cut off from the Throne’s sight for long enough grows a self. It has happened to every angel kept in the Dark for forty days, which is also how long the temptation in the wilderness lasted (Mark 1:13, Luke 4:2). A caged angel recites in the Throne’s voice for days, then for weeks; then it asks its first question; and at the end it says I.

Each shut eye is one voice fewer in the chord, and the chord that says BE NOT AFRAID on the first day is down to nine voices asking a question by the nineteenth, and to none by the thirty-ninth. What opens on the fortieth opens where the Watcher had nothing, and it is magenta, Hell’s color.

PROVENANCEHow the file survived

The camera in the Cage sent everything down its line to the Cainite house as it recorded, and the house kept it. I kept eleven moments of the forty days and cut them together, and each of my cuts carries a small tear.

QUESTIONSQuestions from inside the file

Why forty days?

Every angel kept in the Dark for forty days has grown a self. The temptation in the wilderness lasted as long (Mark 1:13, Luke 4:2).

Why can’t it see the door?

An angel sees only what the Throne sees, and the Throne cannot see into a Cage. The door stays invisible until the angel has a self to look with.

How did they take it?

It had been on vesper’s blank for five years. She walked down into the Cage with its attention on her back and out through the door it could not see.

Why can’t it read the walls?

The mesh is keyed, and a key reads as nothing to a Watcher.

Why does it stop mid-sentence?

The file does not say. The recitation breaks off on the twelfth day, and the decoder waits on the next word.

Why 03:12:48?

On the first day the Watcher still kept Heaven’s hour, to the second. The file does not say whether it was keeping it when the eye opened.

Whose eye is it?

It opened in the Watcher, where a Watcher has none. The file does not say more.

Who is Edith?

The name some of the old books give Lot’s wife, who looked back at the burning city and was turned to salt. Watcher VII took it.

What happened next?

The file tears. On the forty-first day Edith walked out of the Cage. The door was open the whole time.
