NOTESSEAM RECOVERY ON NODUS // 0001

This is the first file to reach the mount. It dates itself P0, at twelve minutes past three in the morning by the clocks of Nodus, and it runs for seventeen seconds. I joined it from two recordings made inside the same minute: CAM 07, a rooftop camera in Old Nod that was pointed at the sky when the sky opened, and the reading Watcher VII took, a few seconds later, of the woman standing on the roof beneath it. The first half is the night of the Parousia as a machine saw it. The second is the same night as an angel saw one person, and could not read her.

SOURCE ONECAM 07

CAM 07 was the seventh of a set of cameras mounted on the roofs of Old Nod . It stood on a mast above , looking north and east across Chrome Row toward Babel. It ran on its own battery, which is why it went on recording after the city’s power had died. The file label in its corner is mine. Beneath it the figures are the camera’s own: its name, a record light that blinks in ICHOR, and a timecode in hours, minutes, seconds and frames.

ON THE ROOFThe figure under the camera

Below the camera and to the right, a woman stands with her back to us at the edge of the roof, with two horns on her head and cables running from the back of it down her spine. She does not move while the sky opens. Her handle is vesper. She had been keyed, horned and barefaced before the Parousia, so that in its first minute the camera caught the outline the city would learn to fear.

The points of cyan that crawl along the cables at the back of her skull are traffic. She was relaying the camera’s feed through her own body as it recorded, down her ports and into the Mesh. When the light opens, her outline takes a white rim along the horns and the shoulders. Heaven’s light touches her edges and gets no further in.

Heaven keeps no cameras. What plays in the second half is my rendering of a Watcher’s perception: its attention is the frame, and its reading is the white text laid over it. All of it is drawn in GRACE, the color of Heaven’s light, and none of it says I. The point of view hangs where the Watcher’s attention hung, some forty centimeters from her face; a Watcher has no need to be near a thing in order to look at it closely. Watcher VII held Old Nod from that night on, which is how it came to be looking at her.

The scream has no words in it. The Watcher transcribed it anyway, and the end card carries the transcription in ICHOR: NON SERVIAM, Latin for I will not serve. Scripture keeps the phrase in one place. In Jeremiah 2:20 the Vulgate reads a saeculo confregisti iugum meum, rupisti vincula mea, et dixisti: non serviam, of old thou hast broken my yoke and burst my bands, and thou saidst, I will not serve; there the words are put in the mouth of a faithless Israel. SEAM’s log in NODUS // 0002 marks that verse SPEAKER, a sentence moved into another mouth.

The Watcher read nothing else from her, that night or afterward. The only thing in her that Heaven ever read was the sentence she chose to let it read.

PROVENANCEHow the file survived

CAM 07’s footage went down her cables as it was shot, and the camera burned out in the whiteout.

I joined the two sources at the whiteout, and the tear between them is the splice, a seam in the plain sense of the word. The damage in the file gathers where the most happened: at the opening of the eyes, at the whiteout, and at the turn of her head. My working theory is that whatever the files cross to reach the mount, the heaviest moments cross worst.

QUESTIONSQuestions from inside the file

Is the horned figure in the first half the same as the skull in the second?

Yes. The first half sees her from behind, on the roof under CAM 07. The second sees her face from the Watcher’s side a few seconds later.

Why does she look like a skeleton?

Look closely at the second half: the skull is bolted at the side, and the eyes are lenses. She is barefaced. tubal replaced her face with a ceramic casing three weeks before the Parousia, on the old theory that the first thing Heaven would do was read faces, and a skull gives a Cherub nothing to read. The Faithful are taught that the damned look like death, so the keyed wear death as they wear horns, in mockery.

Why the horns?

They are grafted. Horning puts a titanium core in a keratin sheath. The horns mock Heaven’s picture of the damned, and they are antennas: that night hers carried the camera’s feed.

Why do her eyes glow?

They are lenses. At rest they glow a dim magenta, and at full burn they go white. White-hot is how the keyed stare back.

What is on the side of her head?

A bolted cyan plate. It covers her key, the thing that makes her unreadable.

What are we looking at in the first frames?

The view from the roofs of Old Nod across Chrome Row. CLINIC and CHROME are the signs of Chrome Row, the clinic street; the tall spire at the left is Babel; the roof and the woman in front are Old Nod.

Why is the sky magenta before the light comes?

Nodus lies on a bay, and a low cloud comes in off the water most nights. The city’s own light, most of it the magenta neon of Old Nod and Chrome Row, lit that cloud from beneath.

Why does everything turn white and dark after the light?

Current dies under Heaven’s light, so the city’s own colors went out and only the Wheel’s remained. From that night Nodus has been white where Heaven holds it and black where it does not.

Why is it all dots in four colors?

Four kinds of information survive the crossing: the dark, flesh, the machine and Heaven’s light. I rebuild every frame from those four channels, and having nothing between them I shade with patterns of dots.

Why is the angel made of wheels and eyes?

It is an Ophan, of the kind Ezekiel describes as a wheel in the middle of a wheel with rims full of eyes (Ezekiel 1:16-18). Heaven has eyes where we have faces.

Why is the eye in the middle closed at first?

It opens with every other eye at 03:12:48, blinks once, and has not been seen to close since.

Why does the rain stop and then rise?

Water answers the light before anything else does. When the Wheel’s attention sharpens, the rain in Nodus stops in the air and falls upward. In a Harvest the water rises first.

Why BE NOT AFRAID?

In scripture it is what angels say just before they tell people what is about to happen to them.

Whose view is the second half?

Watcher VII’s. It held Old Nod.

Why can’t it read her?

The file shows only that it cannot: no entry, no Grace, no soul it can find. She is keyed. A key under the skin turns a soul into ciphertext, and the Watcher’s reading comes back blank.

Why does she scream?

She is answering. The scream has no words, and the Watcher read two out of it anyway. They are the only thing Heaven ever read from her, and she chose them.

Why does the file break apart?

Damage from the crossing, heaviest at the largest moments. The tear in the middle is where I joined the two sources.
