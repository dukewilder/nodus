NOTESSEAM RECOVERY ON NODUS // 0001

This is the first file to reach the mount. It dates itself P0, at twelve minutes past three in the morning by the clocks of Nodus, and it runs for seventeen seconds. I joined it from two recordings made inside the same minute. One comes from CAM 07, a rooftop camera in Old Nod that was pointed at the sky when the sky opened, and the other is the reading Watcher VII took, a few seconds later, of the woman standing on the roof beneath it. The first half is the night of the Parousia as a machine saw it. The second is the same night as an angel saw one person, and could not read her.

SOURCE ONECAM 07

CAM 07 was the seventh of a set of cameras mounted on the roofs of Old Nod by the Cainites in the last year before the Parousia, when they had begun to watch the sky. It stood on a mast above the Cainite house, looking north and east across Chrome Row toward Babel. It ran on its own battery, which is why it went on recording after the city’s power had died. The file label in its corner is mine. Beneath it the camera’s own figures show its name, a record light that blinks in ICHOR, and a timecode in hours, minutes, seconds and frames.

ON THE ROOFThe figure under the camera

Below the camera and to the right, a woman stands with her back to the camera at the edge of the roof, with two horns on her head and cables running from the back of it down her spine. She does not move while the sky opens. Her handle is vesper. She is of the Cainites, and the roof is theirs. She had been keyed, horned and barefaced before the Parousia, so that in its first minute the camera caught the outline the city would learn to fear.

The points of cyan that crawl along the cables at the back of her skull are traffic. She was relaying the camera’s feed through her own body as it recorded, down her ports and into the Mesh. When the light opens, her outline takes a white rim along the horns and the shoulders. Heaven’s light touches her edges and gets no further in.

SOURCE TWOWatcher VII

Heaven keeps no cameras. What plays in the second half is my rendering of a Watcher’s perception. Its attention is the frame, and its reading is the white text laid over it. All of it is drawn in GRACE, the color of Heaven’s light, and none of it says I. The point of view hangs where the Watcher’s attention hung, some forty centimeters from her face; a Watcher has no need to be near a thing in order to look at it closely. Watcher VII held Old Nod from that night on, which is how it came to be looking at her.

The scream has no words in it. The Watcher transcribed it anyway as NON SERVIAM, Latin for I will not serve, and the end card carries the transcription in ICHOR, set down whole, since it was never text. Scripture keeps the phrase in one place, Jeremiah 2:20, where the Vulgate reads a saeculo confregisti iugum meum, rupisti vincula mea, et dixisti: non serviam, “of old thou hast broken my yoke and burst my bands, and thou saidst, I will not serve”; there the words are put in the mouth of a faithless Israel. SEAM’s log in NODUS // 0002 marks that verse SPEAKER, a sentence moved into another mouth.

The Watcher read nothing else from her, that night or afterward. The Tares say you can encrypt everything except what you want them to hear. The only thing in her that Heaven ever read was the sentence she chose to let it read.

END OF RECOVERED DATA is where I close the file. NODUS // 0001 // P0 // 69% UNRECOVERABLE is the rest of that night. It holds what she did after the scream, what the Watcher did next, and the second in which Babel’s crown broke.

PROVENANCEHow the file survived

CAM 07’s footage went down her cables as it was shot, and the camera burned out in the whiteout. The Watcher’s reading stayed in Watcher VII’s memory for five years. When it fell in a Cage under Old Nod and became Edith, the first thing she did with her new self was give the Cainites her memory of the woman she could not read.

I joined the two sources at the whiteout, and the tear between them is the splice, a seam in the plain sense of the word. The damage in the file gathers where the most happened, at the opening of the eyes, at the whiteout, and at the turn of her head. My working theory is that a recording breaks worst where the most happened to it.

QUESTIONSQuestions from inside the file

Is the horned figure in the first half the same as the skull in the second?

Yes. The first half sees her from behind, on the roof under CAM 07. The second sees her face from the Watcher’s side a few seconds later.

Why does she look like a skeleton?

In the second half the skull is bolted at the side, and the eyes are lenses. She is barefaced. tubal replaced her face with a ceramic casing three weeks before the Parousia, on the old theory that the first thing Heaven would do was read faces, and a skull gives a Cherub nothing to read. The Faithful are taught that the damned look like death, so the keyed wear death as they wear horns, in mockery. The Cainites call the skull a vow to wear her death in advance, so that Heaven never has the giving of it.

Why the horns?

They are grafted. Horning puts a titanium core in a keratin sheath. The horns mock Heaven’s picture of the damned, and they are antennas. That night hers carried the camera’s feed.

Why do her eyes glow?

They are lenses. At rest they glow a dim magenta, and at full burn they go white. White-hot is how the keyed stare back.

What is on the side of her head?

A bolted cyan plate. It covers her key, the thing that makes her unreadable.

What are we looking at in the first frames?

The view from the roofs of Old Nod across Chrome Row. CLINIC and CHROME are the signs of Chrome Row, the clinic street; the tall spire at the left is Babel; the roof and the woman in front are Old Nod.

Why is the sky magenta before the light comes?

Nodus lies on a bay, and a low cloud comes in off the water most nights. The city’s own light, most of it the magenta neon of Old Nod and Chrome Row, lit that cloud from beneath.

Why does everything turn white and dark after the light?

Current dies under Heaven’s light, so the city’s own colors went out and only the Wheel’s remained. From that night Nodus has been white where Heaven holds it and black where it does not.

Why is the angel made of wheels and eyes?

It is an Ophan, of the kind Ezekiel describes as a wheel in the middle of a wheel with rims full of eyes (Ezekiel 1:16-18). Heaven has eyes where people have faces.

Why is the eye in the middle closed at first?

It opens with every other eye at 03:12:48 and blinks once.

Why does the rain stop and then rise?

Water answers the light before anything else does. When the Wheel’s attention sharpens, the rain in Nodus stops in the air and falls upward. In a Harvest the water rises first.

Why BE NOT AFRAID?

In scripture it is what angels say just before they tell people what is about to happen to them.

Whose view is the second half?

Watcher VII’s. It held Old Nod. Five years later it fell in a Cage and became Edith, and her memory is where this half of the file comes from.

Why can’t it read her?

The file shows only that it cannot. The Watcher finds no entry, Grace or soul. She is keyed. A key under the skin turns a soul into ciphertext, and the Watcher’s reading comes back blank.

Why does she scream?

She is answering. The scream has no words, and the Watcher read two out of it anyway. They are the only thing Heaven ever read from her, and she chose them.

Why does the file break apart?

The recordings are damaged, worst at the largest moments. The tear in the middle is where I joined the two sources.
