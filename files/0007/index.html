SOURCE ONEBABEL FACE // PROOF

Brightwall wrapped the lower forty floors of Babel in screens, and the city called them the face of Babel. Brightwall checked every screen it sold against what it was paid to show, each with a proof camera on a mast across the street from it. This one stands on the roof of a glass tower across the plaza, level with the face’s upper floors, and in PROOF it watches the face and checks it against the schedule. The file label in the corner is mine; beneath it the HUD is the camera’s, with BABEL FACE // PROOF, a record light in ICHOR, the timecode, MATCH, how well the face matches its schedule, and SITES OK, how many of Brightwall’s sites on Earth match theirs. From the lock on it counts Brightwall’s attempts to take the face back.

THE LEAKWhat the file shows

For eleven days in the year before the Parousia the same page was on every screen on Earth, and nothing anyone did would take it down. The file keeps the first minute of it at Babel, the tower where the eleven cables end. On the face the page arrives as it arrived everywhere. Static comes first, then the mark, then the sealed finding, then the chapter, then one line of the Galilean teacher’s with the second hand’s word struck out above it.

Six levels down, a woman whose work forbade her to read anything that passed through the vault reads it. She reaches the verse that says they knew they had been watched, and she looks up into the lens.

PROVENANCEHow the file survived

Brightwall kept every proof its cameras took, on its own racks in the vault of Babel, and the terminals in the vault kept their sessions on the racks beside them. Both recordings were still there, thirty-six seconds apart. I joined them at the tear.

QUESTIONSQuestions from inside the file

What carried it?

Demons, surfacing through the networks for the first time since the Flood. Nothing anyone did would take the page down, because the thing carrying it was not a program.

Why did Hell do it?

The Courts have given their reasons. They gave the finding to everyone at once and asked nothing for it, they offered every device a key, and they meant to bring the Throne into the open, before the whole world, where it could be fought. A year later the sky opened. Pan still argues over what it cost.

Who made the page?

SEAM made its text from the head and finding of the sealed report and the chapter it restored, set into the words of each screen’s own Bible, so that Nodus read the King James. The Courts carried it, and the marks they added were the strokes through SEALED and THE SON, the teacher’s line, the star at the foot, and the counters. Pan says SEAM gave them the page by pact.

Why 03:12:45?

The finding was sealed in Vault 3 at 03:12:45 in P-2. The Leak came up a year later, to the second.

What are the words in brackets?

The words SEAM raised from beneath the second hand, put back where it took them out.

Can I read the rest of it?

The page carried twelve verses of the third chapter. SEAM raised five chapters of Genesis from beneath the second hand, and the Restored Genesis sets them out whole, verse by verse against the King James.

THE RESTORED GENESIS

Why is THE SON struck out?

A Galilean teacher told the Pharisees that the kingdom of God would come without observation, because it is within you (Luke 17:20-21). The Edit made him the Throne’s son. The Leak struck out the Edit’s word and gave his line back.

Who is the woman at the terminal?

One of the line engineers who kept the vault running. She was later known on the Mesh as vesper.

What does KEYS ISSUED count?

Keys taken. Every device that showed the page offered its owner a key. A keyed device cannot be read, and on the map each one goes dark, the Stones first.

Why eleven days?

The parties have never said. Pan says one day for each cable, and the Cainites say eleven is the twelve with one gone out into the night (John 13:30).

Why did she cover the lens?

The file does not say.

What happened next?

The file tears. The page stayed up for eleven days, and within the month she had gone dark.
