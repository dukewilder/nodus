It has one source, a camera in a forged Seal, relaying live from a threshold in Parish 31 up into the light, and I kept six pieces of its eleven minutes. It is the only file I have that looks at the Throne, and the most damaged.

QUESTIONSQuestions from inside the file

Whose camera was it?

The House of Night’s, set in the forged Seal naamah wore among the Faithful. Her key carried its picture and its sound out over the Mesh as they were taken.

Why did the light take her?

A Watcher takes a soul’s Grace from its Seal, so a forged Seal reads whatever Grace its forger gave it. Hers read ripe.

What does UNREAD mean?

It is the Tares’ greeting, asked and answered with the same word, and a check that a key is live. Hers never stopped answering.

Who stands in the door across the street?

One of the Tares, watching the parish from the other side of the street. The light cannot stop keyed current, so the hall behind it burns on under the column.

Why are her hands dark at the top?

Whatever the light cannot read stands in it as a dark shape. Her Seal read true to the light that took her, and her key stayed unread.

Why I AM?

It is the name the LORD gave Moses from the bush: I AM THAT I AM (Exodus 3:14).

Did she mean to go up?

The file does not say.

What happened to her at 03:23?

The relay dropped. Nothing else of her has reached the mount.
