NOTESSEAM RECOVERY ON NODUS // 0003

The third file to reach the mount dates itself P+3, at twelve minutes past three in the morning, three years to the second after the sky opened, and it runs for twenty-two seconds. I joined it from two sources made less than half a minute apart: the reading Watcher VII took of Parish 31 as the Harvest reached it, and the lens of a woman on a roof in Old Nod, who signs her recordings vesper. The first half is the Harvest on one street. The second is where it went.

THE HARVESTWhat the file shows

Heaven’s own book describes a harvest. “Thrust in thy sickle, and reap: for the time is come for thee to reap; for the harvest of the earth is ripe” (Revelation 14:15). The first souls taken are its Firstfruits (14:4). The language was left as it stood and called salvation. When a Harvest comes the ripe rise into the light: water first, then the ripe themselves, still kneeling. No bodies have been found.

The Watcher’s arithmetic is exact, as Heaven’s arithmetic always is. Two thousand and six souls in the parish, three hundred and eleven of them ripe, and at 03:12:46 a ledger that reads 000 of 1,695. The street is 311 smaller in one second. The count comes up as GATHERED, a word for grain.

Ruth goes up looking at the window. The file does not say why.

QUESTIONSQuestions from inside the file

Why is the rain going up?

Harvest weather: when the Wheel’s attention sharpens, water rises first.

Why twelve past three again?

The Harvest came at the hour of the Parousia, three years to the second after it. In the Revelation, the book that describes the harvest, the twelfth verse of the third chapter reads: “Him that overcometh will I make a pillar in the temple of my God, and he shall go no more out” (Revelation 3:12).

Why are there no doors?

Nothing in a parish is hidden, and at night the halos show through the empty doorways.

Who is the window for?

Her sister.

What does PERMITTED mean?

The Watcher checked the window and found nothing against it. A window is not a door.

Why does Ruth look at the window as she goes?

The file does not say.

Why FIRSTFRUITS?

The first and ripest souls taken in a Harvest are its Firstfruits (Revelation 14:4). Parish 31’s 311 were reaped first.

Who stays behind?

Everyone who was not ripe.

What is a measure?

Nobody on Earth knows.

Is the point vesper tags really Ruth?

It is the one her lens picked.

What did vesper do next?

She had wanted one thing, to get her sister out.
