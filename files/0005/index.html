The fifth file to reach the mount dates itself P0, at twelve minutes past three in the morning of the fortieth day after the sky opened, and it runs for twenty-four seconds. Both of its sources are one camera, CAM 13, which the Cainites set on a roof in the East Wards at the end of one of the Great Avenues. The first is the timelapse it took of the uptown by night through those forty days. The second is the same camera live on the fortieth, turned onto a wall at the White’s edge. The first half is the Consecration. The second is what it did to someone who was in its way.

SOURCE ONECAM 13 // TIMELAPSE

CAM 13 is the thirteenth of the Cainites’ cameras. The other twelve stand on the roofs of Old Nod, pointed at the sky. This one stands on a roof in the East Wards and looks down one of the Great Avenues into the uptown through a long lens: the avenue running away down the middle of the frame to Babel’s broken spire, the towers of the new money on either side of it, and the Wheel over Babel in its opening in the cloud. It takes its frames by night, from dusk to dawn, since by day the Wheel’s light burns out a sensor, as it burned out CAM 07’s. The file label in the corner is mine; beneath it the HUD is the camera’s: CAM 13 // TIMELAPSE, a record light in ICHOR, the hour each frame was taken, the day, and the battery. Of the forty nights I recovered thirty-five, a few frames from most of them and more from the last. The fourth, the ninth, the twelfth, the eighteenth and the twenty-third are lost.

The end card is the verse the stone is named for. Heaven calls the stone Consecration, after the twenty-ninth chapter of Exodus, where the priests and the altar are consecrated over seven days, and this verse is the last of that chapter’s instructions for it. It was text long before the archive had it, so I type it at my own speed and hold before SHALL BE HOLY. It is set in GRACE, as Heaven speaks, with its chapter and verse under it.

THE CONSECRATIONWhat the file shows

The stone takes a building when everyone who lives in it has knelt, so the timelapse is a count. Each tower goes white on the night its count comes out even, and the camera keeps the order: the far end first, then the church, then the rest down the avenue toward the camera, and last a tower on the right that held out on one window.

The stone never takes the avenue, since nobody lived on it to kneel, and in the Wards along the foot of the frame the lamps stay lit all forty nights.

The live half is the stone near enough to see, on the fortieth day, at twelve minutes past three. It has closed over someone who was leaning on a wall, and it is still coming up over their sleeve when the battery gives out.

PROVENANCEHow the file survived

CAM 13 sent every frame down its line to the Cainite house in Old Nod as it took it, and the house kept them. The live recording came down the same line until the battery gave out. I joined the two at the tear.
