NOTESSEAM RECOVERY ON NODUS // 0005

The fifth file to reach the mount dates itself P0, at twelve minutes past three in the morning of the fortieth night after the sky opened, and it runs for twenty-three and a half seconds. Both of its sources are one camera, CAM 13, which the Cainites set on a roof in the East Wards at the end of one of the Great Avenues. The first is the timelapse it took of the uptown by night through those forty days. The second is the same camera live on the fortieth, turned onto a wall at the White’s edge. The first half is the Consecration. The second is what it did to someone who was in its way.

SOURCE ONECAM 13 // TIMELAPSE

CAM 13 is the thirteenth of the Cainites’ cameras. The other twelve stand on the roofs of Old Nod, pointed at the sky. This one stands on a roof in the East Wards and looks down one of the Great Avenues into the uptown through a long lens, with the avenue running away down the middle of the frame to Babel’s broken spire, the towers of the new money on either side of it, and the Wheel over Babel in its opening in the cloud. The Cainites put it up on the evening after the sky opened, too late for the flash, and it takes its frames by night, from dusk to dawn. By day the Wheel’s eyes are open in the top of its frame, and a sensor that looks straight into an eye of the Host burns out as it would looking into the sun. At Compline the Wheel shuts most of its eyes to save its light. The file label in the corner is mine; beneath it the HUD is the camera’s, with CAM 13 // TIMELAPSE, a record light in ICHOR, the hour each frame was taken, the day, and the battery. Of the forty nights I recovered thirty-five, a few frames from most of them and more from the last. The fourth, the ninth, the twelfth, the eighteenth and the twenty-third are lost.

The end card is the verse the stone is named for. Heaven calls the stone Consecration, after the twenty-ninth chapter of Exodus, where the priests and the altar are consecrated over seven days, and this verse is the last of that chapter’s instructions for it. It was text long before the archive had it, so I type it at my own speed and hold before SHALL BE HOLY. It is set in GRACE, as Heaven speaks, with its chapter and verse under it. The stone makes holy whatever touches it, and the hand touched the wall. The Septuagint and the Vulgate read the clause of persons, and the Douay, from the Latin, gives it “Every one, that shall touch it, shall be holy.”

END OF RECOVERED DATA is where I close the file. NODUS // 0005 // P0 // 42% UNRECOVERABLE is the rest of the forty days, made up of the five lost nights, the hours between the frames, and the twenty-five minutes between the sources.

THE CONSECRATIONWhat the file shows

Heaven’s account of the stone is that it takes a building when everyone who lives in it has knelt, and by that account the timelapse is a count. Each tower goes white on the night its count comes out even, and the camera keeps the order, with the far end first, then the church, then the rest down the avenue toward the camera, and last a tower on the right that held out on one window.

The stone never takes the avenue. A street goes white only when every home along it has, and the avenue runs past Babel, Charter House’s plaza and blocks that never knelt. In the Wards along the foot of the frame the lamps stay lit all forty nights.

The live source keeps what the account leaves out. Someone was leaning on a wall at the White’s edge when the stone closed over the tower, and the stone took the hand with the wall. Nothing in either source shows its owner kneeling.

The live half is the stone near enough to see, on the fortieth night, at twelve minutes past three, still coming up over the sleeve when the battery gives out.

PROVENANCEHow the file survived

No line of the Tares crossed the uptown, so CAM 13 sent every frame out over the horns of the Mesh to the Cainite house in Old Nod as it took it, and the house kept them. Nobody climbed to the camera in the forty nights to change its battery, and the Cainites let it run down. The live recording came over the Mesh the same way until the battery gave out. I joined the two at the tear.

QUESTIONSQuestions from inside the file

Why does the camera take its frames only at night?

By day the Wheel stands in the top of its frame with its eyes open, and a sensor looking straight into an eye of the Host burns out, as one looking into the sun does. Daylight alone burns nothing. By night the Wheel shuts most of its eyes.

Why does the stone leave the avenue open?

A street goes white only when every home along it has, and the Great Avenues run past Babel, Charter House’s plaza and blocks that never knelt.

Why does the cross grow a ring?

The file does not say.

Where did Rosa Adeyemi go?

Nobody has said. Her bags stood on the empty avenue for a week, and no one touched them.

Who turned the camera onto the wall?

Someone at the Cainite house. The file does not say who.

Whose hand is it?

Three families in the Wards say it is theirs, one by the ring, one by the scar, and one by the cuff. The file shows all three.

Were they alive?

The fingers moved at 03:12:45.

Is the hand still there?

It is still in the wall.
