THE BAREFACINGWhat the file shows

A face is the first thing anything reads. The camera over tubal’s chair was built to find one and keep it, and it does its work well: it holds her face through the lifting, follows it into the dish, and goes on calling it a face at fourteen percent. When it looks back at the chair it finds a skull, a crucifix hung the wrong way up, a star on the wall, and nobody.

The procedure has a name among the Tares. The face comes off and a ceramic skull goes on, so that whatever reads faces has nothing to read, and the one who wears it is barefaced. tubal’s word for it is shorter.

Twenty-one minutes later her new lenses find the ones who were going to do the reading, in a ring over the city, three weeks early.

PROVENANCEHow the file survived

The table camera kept every procedure on a keyed store in the clinic, and tubal took the store with him when the clinics of the Row went under the street. Her lenses kept their own recording. I joined the two at the tear.

QUESTIONSQuestions from inside the file

Why take her face?

The Cainites’ old theory was that the first thing Heaven would do was read faces. The Cherub gates raised after the Parousia read four things, and the first of them is the face.

tubal. He hangs his tools on it.

Who are the watchers?

What happened to the face?

What does NOBODY mean?

The camera had just counted no faces. To anything that reads faces, she is nobody.
