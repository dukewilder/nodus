A Locket, the city’s phone, made to answer to its owner and nobody else. The file keeps the first of those nights from inside the pile, in the hall where the Lockets were given up, on the plaza where one woman stood while everyone else knelt, and in the fire.

QUESTIONSQuestions from inside the file

Whose Locket was it?

The file never shows its owner. The parishes keep no current, so somebody charged it somewhere else, set it recording and laid it in the pile with its lens out.

Why did the parishes burn their Lockets?

The Directive in force called in every lock, key, cipher, password, veil and sealed vessel in the parishes, because a thing that cannot be read has been hidden. Every Locket answered to its owner and nobody else. Directive 10 burns what is surrendered at the Burn on the sixth day of every week, citing the books burned at Ephesus (Acts 19:19).

THE COVENANT WHOLE

What is the Lamb?

Heaven’s image of the Son, standing as it had been slain, with seven horns and seven eyes (Revelation 5:6). It stands in the light of every sealed window in the city.

Who is the woman who stands?

Dr. Livia Sarn, the Director of the Palimpsest Institute, by whose order SEAM’s finding was sealed. After the Leak she recanted on every channel that would carry her.

Why the salt?

Heaven holds no trial. Under the Pillar the reading is the verdict, and the sentence comes in the Choir’s voice through the nearest Faithful mouth, as the verse of Lot’s wife. She was convicted of authorship. Heaven counts no mind a maker, so it charged the one who ran the Institute.

Who are the gardeners?

The Company’s gardeners, who planted the Charter Gardens and were kept on when the Gardens became the Salt.

What looks back from the fire?

The file does not say.

What happened next?

The fire burned until Lauds. The gardeners set the Director in the first plot of the first Salt Garden, facing the parishes, and the rain has worn her down to the height of a child since. The Tares leave keys at her foot.
