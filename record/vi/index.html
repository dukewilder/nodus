The Cainites

About the year 180 Irenaeus of Lyon condemned the Cainites for honoring Cain and Esau, Korah, the men of Sodom and Judas, and for keeping a Gospel of Judas (Against Heresies 1.31); and a Gospel of Judas did turn up, in a codex out of the Egyptian desert, in the 1970s. The Cainites themselves never went away. They kept their tradition underground for eighteen centuries, and they kept one secret, which was where Nod had been.
