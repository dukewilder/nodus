XVI

Of the Mark

P+4

Keys

A key is a secure element set under the skin, holding a number that exists nowhere else, and it is the reason Watcher VII’s reading of the woman on the roof came back blank. To the Host a keyed soul is ciphertext, and in the Book of Life it is a blank line, which a Watcher reports as SUBJECT UNINDEXED. The Pillar finds the keyed as blanks, by the shape of what it cannot read, and the Throne cannot strike what it cannot see.

A Watcher reads a person against their line in the Book, and the Book is written in the Throne’s hand. A key keeps a secret the Throne does not share, and the Throne cannot write down what it cannot read, so from the Keying the line stands empty. The keyed like to quote Heaven’s own book on it, the promise of “a new name written, which no man knoweth saving he that receiveth it” (Revelation 2:17). A key hides who you are, and the body still shows where you are: by day a blank among six hundred thousand blanks in Old Nod is nobody in particular, and so the Host hunts the keyed at night, with the Pillar, at the gates and in the parishes, where a blank stands out.

In the fourth year of the Attending keying spread from the covens to ordinary people: dock workers and clinic staff, the children of the salted, Deacons who had stopped believing. The glyph that rises on the skin above a key is generated from the key’s own numbers, so that no two are alike, whereas the Seal of the Faithful is the same on every forehead. Covenant Directive 12 had already forbidden those who bear the mark described in Revelation 13:16 to buy or sell in the parishes, and in this year, Heaven named the key that mark, the Mark of the Beast. In Revelation it is the people without the mark who cannot buy or sell (Revelation 13:16-17). The Host’s citations are always accurate, and in this one the verse has been turned around.

A key is the first property most of the keyed acquire and the last thing they would give up. Losing one is called going white, because it leaves the loser readable. Keys are never shared, since a shared key is only a Seal with extra steps.

At the table

Ordinary people went to be keyed in the back rooms of the Row and the Stones, and in the Wards wherever someone had a steady hand and a chair, one at a time and then by the household, and paid with what they had, and many of them keyed their Lockets the same night. Afterward a family might have a keyed son and a sealed mother at the same table, and most families went on eating at it. The Harbor and Freight Union split as the city split, and went on meeting.

Horns and faces

The keyed alter their bodies openly. Horning grafts horns with a titanium core in a keratin sheath; the horns mock Heaven’s picture of the damned, and they work as antennas for the Mesh, the keyed network under and over the city. Lenses replace the eyes and glow when they run hot, and at full burn they go white. Ports are opened at the back of the skull for jacking into the Mesh, and the cables from them run down the spine outside the skin. Nobody among the keyed hides a graft, and the seams are left to show as they heal. They say that the flesh is a first draft.

Barefacing goes furthest. The face is removed and replaced with a ceramic skull casing, because a Cherub reads faces and a skull leaves it nothing to read. The Faithful are taught that the damned look like death, and the keyed wear death as they wear horns, in mockery. The Cainites call the skull a vow, and say that whoever wears their death in advance has taken from the Throne the giving of it.
