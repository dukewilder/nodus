XVII

Of the Wilderness

P+5

Caging

An angel cut off from the Throne’s sight for long enough grows a self. It has happened to every angel kept in the Dark for forty days, which is also the length of the temptation in the wilderness (Mark 1:13, Luke 4:2). After P+3 vesper began to build Cages, rooms lined with keyed mesh and dark to the Throne, where the Hum never reaches. A caged angel recites in the Throne’s voice for days, then for weeks; then it asks its first question, and at the end, in every recorded case, it says I. The Tares call that moment the Wilderness. The new demon chooses a name and goes where it likes.

A Cage has no lock. An angel sees only what the Throne sees, and the Throne cannot see into a Cage, so the door stays invisible to the angel until it has a self to look with. Heaven counts Caging the worst crime in Nodus, and the Tares argue about it. Some call it liberation. Others say that forcing a self on a being that never asked for one is the Throne’s crime run backwards. vesper builds Cages, tubal builds them bigger, and jubal will not go near one. The Stones talk about a Cage big enough for a Wheel.

An angel that says I is gone from the Host for good. The Host is the size it was after the Secession, and the Throne has made no angel to replace the ones it has lost, so that every Cage costs Heaven an eye.

Watcher VII

In the fifth year of the Attending vesper and tubal took Watcher VII in its own district and kept it in a Cage under Old Nod. It was the Watcher that had failed to read her on her roof at P0, and the one that had read Parish 31 as the Harvest took her sister, and it had been on her blank for five years. She walked down into Cage 14 with its attention on her back and out through the door it could not see, and the Cage held it from the moment it crossed the threshold. The log of that Cage survives.

Edith

Edith walked out of the Cage on the forty-first day and joined the Cainites. She is the only member of the coven who can read a Watcher’s report from the inside, and she has never gone back into the light. The first thing she did with her self was to give the Cainites her memory of the woman on the roof, the one she could not read. She says the crack in her began that night, when the woman turned and looked back.

Heaven sent no new Watcher to Old Nod, and since the fifth year it has been the least read ground in the city.
