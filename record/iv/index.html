IV

Of Cain and the First City

THE LAND OF NOD

The two offerings

Two brothers brought offerings. Abel brought the firstlings of his flock and the fat thereof, and Cain brought of the fruit of the ground he had worked, “and the LORD had respect unto Abel and to his offering: but unto Cain and to his offering he had not respect” (Genesis 4:3-5). The blood was accepted and the work refused. SEAM’s log in Vault 3 marks the verse that records the acceptance GIFT, and the verse in which the LORD asks Cain where his brother is CREDIT.

The mark

Scripture says that the LORD set a mark upon Cain, lest any finding him should kill him (Genesis 4:15). The Cainite tradition says that Cain cut the mark himself, a sign the Throne could neither read nor remove, and that it is the only reason he lived. It was the first key. The mark held a name Cain gave himself and told nobody, cut as a glyph only he could read; the Throne reads a soul against the line it wrote for it in the Book, and the name under the mark matched nothing it had written, so from the day he cut it Cain’s line read blank.

The first city

“And Cain went out from the presence of the LORD, and dwelt in the land of Nod, on the east of Eden. And Cain knew his wife; and she conceived, and bare Enoch: and he builded a city, and called the name of the city, after the name of his son, Enoch” (Genesis 4:16-17). It is the first city in scripture, and the man who built it was the first man God cursed. The city on the bay has two names. Heaven’s records call it Nodus, which is Latin for a knot, and everyone who lives there calls it Nod, which is the older name by some thousands of years.

Within six generations Cain’s line had produced every art in Genesis. Jabal “was the father of such as dwell in tents, and of such as have cattle,” which is to say of trade; Jubal “was the father of all such as handle the harp and organ”; and Tubal-cain was “an instructer of every artificer in brass and iron,” and his sister was Naamah (Genesis 4:20-22). The favored line of Seth produced the verse “then began men to call upon the name of the LORD” (Genesis 4:26), and nothing else that anyone remembers. The Cainites of Nodus take their handles from this line.
