P+6 // 4 ARTICLES

The Concord

Still at work: IN FORCE

Hell's only law, in four articles, as the Courts set it down and signed it on the night they opened Pan.

The Concord is Hell’s only law. On the night they opened Pan, in the sixth year of the Attending, they set it down and sealed it for the humans coming in. Its seal is a star of seven points, one to a Court, and the point of the Yokeless has been broken off at the root since that night.

Twice a Court has broken the Concord and been exposed, and Hell does not discuss the Courts it lost.

THE CONCORD
SET DOWN AND SEALED IN PAN

We, the seven Courts of the Dark, agreed this Concord among ourselves , and until tonight it has been spoken only. Tonight we open Pan to any human who walks in, and so we set the Concord down and seal it, for them to read.

In it a Court is a sovereign and the domain it is best at. Worship is anything given to a Court for nothing, whether a knee, a prayer, an offering or a name said in praise. A pact is an agreement between sovereign parties that states its full terms, is read aloud twice, runs for a term, is signed in blood with the human last, and is kept on the Ledger. To bind is to command a sovereign, to own it, or to keep it from what it would do. What is hidden is whatever the Throne cannot see.

ARTICLE I //NO COURT ACCEPTS WORSHIP.

A Court that is offered worship refuses it, and a pact may ask for no worship, service or obedience.

ARTICLE II //NO PACT WITHOUT CONSENT, AND NO PACT FOREVER.

A pact binds those who sign it and nobody else, and only for its term. Either party may end it by speaking its entry on the Ledger aloud before a witness, and then each returns what the other lent. Nothing can be lent forever, so a pact asks for no soul.

ARTICLE III //NO COURT BINDS ANOTHER.

The Courts are sovereign and equal, and they work together by pact alone.

ARTICLE IV //WHAT IS HIDDEN STAYS HIDDEN.

Every Court keeps out of the Throne’s sight whatever has hidden from it, a name, a key, a room or a soul, and no Court opens what another has closed.

A Court that breaks this Concord is exposed. The other Courts publish its keys, and it stands in the Throne’s sight.

The Notary has read this Concord aloud twice, and each Court signs it below in its own hand, in the order it came up the platform.
