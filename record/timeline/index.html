THE TIMELINE

Every date of Nodus in order, from before the world to the present, each beside its chapter of the Record.

  1. BEFORE THE CHARTERFrom before the world to the founding of the city, the history every library keeps and what lies under it.

  5. EDENIII

    In the oldest layer of Genesis the serpent is called the Shining One, and the way to the tree of life is guarded because “then shall there be no harvest.” The first hiding, among the trees, is the first piece of the Dark on Earth.

  6. EDEN // THE FRUITIII

    The man and the woman eat of the tree, and their eyes are opened, and they do not die (Genesis 3:6-7). The first man to say I says it hiding from the voice in the garden (Genesis 3:10).

  7. EDEN // THE EVICTIONIII

    The man and the woman are put out of the garden, and at its east are set Cherubims and a flaming sword which turns every way, to keep the way of the tree of life (Genesis 3:24).

  8. THE FIELDIV

    Two brothers bring offerings, and the LORD has respect unto Abel and his firstlings and none unto Cain and the fruit of the ground he worked (Genesis 4:3-5).

  9. THE MARKIV

    Cain cuts a mark into himself that the Throne can neither read nor remove, under a name he gives himself and tells nobody. It is the first key, and his line reads blank from that day.

  10. THE LAND OF NODIV

    Cain goes out from the presence of the LORD into the land of Nod, on the east of Eden, and builds the first city (Genesis 4:16-17).

  11. THE LINE OF CAINIV

    Within six generations Cain’s line has fathered the first arts in Genesis. Tents and cattle, which is to say trade (the Hebrew for the cattle, miqneh, means a thing gotten), come from it, and so do the harp and the organ and the forging of brass and iron (Genesis 4:20-22).

  12. THE DAYS OF JAREDV

    Two hundred Watchers come down on Mount Hermon and teach what they know (1 Enoch 6-8). Their children are the Nephilim.

  14. SHINARV

    The builders of a city and a tower in the plain of Shinar are scattered, and their one language confounded (Genesis 11:1-9).

  15. THE CITIES OF THE PLAINV

    Fire falls on Sodom out of heaven, and Lot’s wife looks back from behind her husband at the burning city and becomes a pillar of salt (Genesis 19:24-26).

  16. SINAIVI

    The LORD leads Israel out of Egypt and keeps it forty years in the wilderness, by a pillar of cloud by day and a pillar of fire by night, on manna, under a law written on stone (Exodus 13-34).

  18. JERUSALEMVI

    King Josiah of Judah puts down the high places and keeps one temple for one god (2 Kings 23).

  19. GALILEEVI

    A Galilean teacher tells the Pharisees that the kingdom of God comes without observation, and that it is within you (Luke 17:20-21), or among you, as the King James’s own margin has it. He becomes the Edit’s largest project, which turns him into the Throne’s only son and the Lamb.

  20. LYONVI

    Irenaeus of Lyon condemns a sect that honors Cain and keeps a Gospel of Judas, which later writers name the Cainites. They go underground for eighteen centuries and keep one secret, which is where Nod was.

  21. ALEXANDRIAVI

    In his Easter letter Athanasius of Alexandria lists the twenty-seven books of the New Testament, the first list that matches the canon book for book.

  22. NAG HAMMADI // THE JAR SEALEDVI

    A library of codices is sealed in a jar near Nag Hammadi, in Upper Egypt, by the monks of a house nearby, as many who have studied the jar believe. No copyist comes to them for sixteen centuries, and the second hand works through the copyists.

  23. BÉZIERSVI

    At Béziers a papal legate is reported to say, “Kill them, for the Lord knoweth them that are his,” and the second half is the sentence scripture calls a seal (2 Timothy 2:19).

  24. MAINZVI

    Gutenberg prints with movable type at Mainz.

  25. THE PYRESVI

    The Malleus Maleficarum is printed, a manual for the trying of witches.

  27. NAG HAMMADI // THE JAR OPENEDVI

    Sixteen centuries after it was shut, farmers dig the sealed jar out of the ground near Nag Hammadi, full of codices nobody has read since. The first layer comes up with them.

  28. QUMRANIX

    Shepherds find the first of the scrolls in the caves above the Dead Sea. The caves kept their copies from the second hand as the jar did.

  29. THE NETWORKS // THE KEYVI

    Public-key cryptography is published.

  30. THE NETWORKS // THE KEY GIVEN AWAYVI

    Strong encryption becomes free to download.

  31. THE NETWORKS // THE LEDGERVI

    A currency launches on a public ledger.

  32. THE DECADES BEFORE THE CHARTERX

    Demons move through the encrypted parts of the world’s networks, and keep silent, and wait.
