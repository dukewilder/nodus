XX

Of the Babel Line

P+9

Open war

In the ninth year of the Attending the war came into the open. At Matins the fifth Trumpet sounded, and the Seraphim came down, with six wings each, burning with no source of heat, and the front formed inside Babel, whose vault holds the cables that join Pan to the undercities of the world. Heaven holds the upper floors from the broken crown down, and the Tares hold the vault and the lower floors, where the cables are; between them is the Babel Line, a few floors of burned slab that move up or down by the week. The Seraphim burn through the floors rather than use the stairs. The Tares fight in the stairwells, with black steel, which is the only thing that wounds a manifested angel; against a Seraph a gun is noise.

The stairwells they fight in are the ones the Exchange’s night cleaners walked down on the night of the Parousia, as many as a hundred and eighty floors in the dark, and they still go by the cleaners’ names for them.

A Seraph cut with black steel goes out all at once, like a lamp. It had no self, so nothing of it goes on.

At Lauds the sixth Trumpet sounded, and Michael came down. His name is a question, Who is like God?, to which one answer is permitted; he won the War in Heaven. The commonest graffiti in Old Nod is his name translated, with its reply, WHO IS LIKE GOD? I AM. The same day the Wheel dropped two hundred meters, the first time it had moved in nine years.

Every year of the Attending had made Nodus harder to read. Pan had gone deeper, the keyed had passed two million, and since the fifth year Old Nod had had no Watcher. The Tares say that the harder the Landlord stares at Nodus, the deeper Pan goes, and the Wheel’s descent was the other half of the saying, for it came two hundred meters closer to read.
