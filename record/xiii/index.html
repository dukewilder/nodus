XIII

The parishes

The Terraces became Zion Heights, and the Faithful call it the Pews. Its streets were divided into numbered parishes of about two thousand souls, each run by a Deacon; the Deacons are human, and the good ones are kind. At every Hour the Faithful kneel wherever they are, on their own thresholds before dawn, and a halo burns about every head, as bright as its owner’s Grace. Parish homes run without electricity or devices, and the only music in them is the Hum.

The Directives

Heaven publishes its law as the law was published at Sinai, on stone, “written with the finger of God” (Exodus 31:18). A Directive appears cut into the wall of every parish hall at once, and a Deacon reads it aloud at the next Hour.

The Covenant runs to twelve Directives, each with its verse. The first four came together at Lauds on the first day of the first year of the Attending, and the fourth made the city’s churches and meeting halls the parish halls; the rest followed through the year.

  1. On the Seal (Revelation 7:3): every soul in Nodus to be sealed in its parish.
  2. On the Hours (Psalm 119:62, 164): the eight Hours kept kneeling, and the curfew from Compline to Lauds.
  3. On Grace (2 Corinthians 9:7): every soul that bears the Seal scored, and the ripe promised the Harvest.
  4. On the Parishes (Acts 6:3): the city numbered into parishes of about two thousand souls, each under a Deacon.
  8. On the Gates (Genesis 3:24): a Cherub on the streets between districts.

The gates

Movement between districts goes through Cherub gates, which read four things: face, voice, gait and heartbeat. A Cherub has four faces, of a man, a lion, an ox and an eagle, and a turning sword, and one stands in every gate. The sealed walk into the light of the frame one at a time, saying the surrender, not my will but thine, as they go, and the Cherub reads all four against the Book of Life. A sealed soul it reads walks on, and an unsealed one it sends back. A soul it cannot read stops in the frame, held in the light, until the Wardens come for it, . So the Faithful pass, and everyone else goes under.
