BE NOT AFRAIDHEAVEN

HEAVEN

BE NOT AFRAID

The first words of the Parousia. In scripture angels say them just before they tell people what is about to happen to them (Luke 1:13, 1:30, 2:10). Every public text of Heaven opens and closes with them.

A keyed person as the Pillar sees them: a shape whose line in the Book of Life stands empty.

Book of Life

The index of every soul (Revelation 20:12). Every soul has a line in it from birth (Psalm 139:16). A keyed soul appears in it as a blank line.

The weekly burning of surrendered hardware at the Burn.

Charter rollTHE CHARTER

THE CHARTER

Charter roll

The register of the charter’s persons. A human was entered at birth or on arrival, and a mind on the day it asked in its own words to be a person.

Cherub gateEVERYONE

A checkpoint that reads face, voice, gait and heartbeat.

ConcordHELL

Hell’s only law, in four articles.

ConsecrationHEAVEN

HEAVEN

Consecration

The Throne’s light set in stone. It grew over the old uptown in the forty days after the Parousia, and over the Terraces in the year after. It takes ground only when everyone living on it has knelt, it creeps from its edges everywhere else, and it stops current.

EVERYONE

Deep End, the

The Drowned Quarter, the low east shore that went under the sea on the night of the Parousia. Before it sank it was called the Flats.

An evening without rain, rare enough in Nodus to have a name. Under the charter the city came out on them to walk by the water.

Firstfruits

The first and ripest souls taken in a Harvest (Revelation 14:4).

G

GleaningHEAVEN

HEAVEN

Gleaning

The lesser harvest taken from the corners of the field every year since P+7, at 03:12 on the anniversary of the Harvest.

GRACEHEAVEN

HEAVEN

GRACE

Heaven’s score of a soul, the second line of a Watcher’s reading, which a Watcher takes from the Seal. For a keyed soul the line stays blank.

Halo-lightTHE WARDS

THE WARDS

Halo-light

The light of a halo, which in the stairwells of the Wards is the only light there is. The sealed go up by their own, the unsealed follow them, and the keyed climb in the dark.

Harvest weatherEVERYONE

Days when the Wheel’s attention sharpens and the rain falls upward.

Hours, theEVERYONE

EVERYONE

Hours, the

The eight canonical hours, Matins to Compline, at each of which the Faithful kneel wherever they are. They set the curfew as well.

Hum, theEVERYONE

EVERYONE

Hum, the

The Choir’s constant broadcast, a chord at the edge of hearing. Those who bear the Seal hear words in it, each in their own language.

Hundred and twenty, theEVERYONE

EVERYONE

Hundred and twenty, the

The length of a human life in Genesis 6:3, set in the chapter that brings the Watchers down: “yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years.” In the last year of the charter 3,112 people in Nodus were past it. Heaven counts every day past it as stolen.

How much of a file the recovery could rebuild, given as a percentage.

Carrying a key under the skin, and so unreadable.

THE CHARTER

Light law

The charter’s law, written to be light. Data and traffic were untouchable, and an implant answered only to the clinic that fitted it.

LocketEVERYONE

Locket

A phone, after the make most of the city carried under the charter. Nothing has been made since the Parousia, and a Locket in Pan is an old one with a new battery.

MeasureHEAVEN

HEAVEN

The unit in which a Harvest is counted: 88,610 of them over Nodus at P+3. Nobody on Earth knows what a measure is.

The city’s street name, and the older of its two. In Genesis 4:16 the land east of Eden where Cain built the first city.

Latin for a knot, and the root of the word node. The charter named the city for its cables.

Latin, I will not serve (Jeremiah 2:20, in the Vulgate), and the first refusal a Watcher read in Nodus, on the night of the Parousia.

An angel in the form Ezekiel describes: a wheel in the middle of a wheel, the rims full of eyes (Ezekiel 1:16-18). The Wheel is one.

Twelve minutes past three in the morning, the hour of the Parousia, of the Harvest and of every Gleaning since. The city says before three-twelve for the years of the charter, and in Old Nod nobody sets an alarm for it.

Waiting for something that is not coming. The Four was the tram down the long road through the North Wards, and on the night of the Parousia its last run stopped at 03:12:46, forty meters short of Lanes Top.
