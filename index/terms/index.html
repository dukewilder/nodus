PactHELL, THE TARES

HELL, THE TARES

Pact

A contract between sovereign parties. The Courts cooperate with one another by pact and deal with everyone else the same way. A pact states its full terms, is read aloud twice by the Notary or one of his clerks, and runs for a fixed term, since the Concord forbids forever. It is signed in blood, since a genome is the one signature nobody can forge, the human signs last, and it goes onto the Ledger. Either party ends it by speaking its Ledger entry aloud before a witness, and each then returns what the other lent. A pact never asks for worship, for obedience, for anyone who has not signed, or for a soul.

Wards, the

The housing the Charter Company put up fast in the boom years, north and west of the Terraces and east beyond the Flats, block after block on grids of its own, numbered and then named by the street: the Kilns, the Sidings, Nine Acres, Westfield and the Top Road in the North Wards, and Turbine Row, the Tanks, Airside and Longacre in the East. They held half the city at the census and hold more than half of it now. Most of their people took the Seal, and every block holds someone who refused or someone keyed, so the stone has never come up under them. Nobody holds them, the Tares run no lines there, and their rooms are lit a few hours a night on batteries charged in the dark.

Wheat

The Tares’ word for the Faithful, from the parable Heaven named the Tares from, in which the householder tells the reapers to gather the wheat into his barn (Matthew 13:30). The Faithful have taken the word up, and say they would rather be good wheat.

Wheel, the

The Ophan of Nodus. At the Parousia it took station nine hundred meters above the center of the city, beside Babel, and held it for nine years without moving a meter. At P+9 it dropped two hundred meters, and it has hung there since, seven hundred meters over the city.

Whiteout, the

The full light of the Wheel at 03:12:50 on the night of the Parousia, two seconds after it read the city. It burned out CAM 07’s sensor, and at 03:12:51 it struck Babel’s crown and broke off the top forty floors. Nodus has not been lit so brightly since.
