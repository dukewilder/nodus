ConcordHELL

HELL

Concord

Hell’s only law. Its first article says that no Court accepts worship, its second that no pact is made without consent and none forever, its third that no Court binds another, and its fourth that what is hidden stays hidden. A Court that breaks it is exposed, its keys published and itself left standing in the Throne’s sight. That has happened twice, and Hell does not discuss the Courts it lost. The Courts agreed it among themselves at the end of the Secession. They set it down on the night they opened Pan, in P+6, for the humans coming in, and signed it there under a seal of seven points, one to a Court, whose Yokeless point has been broken off at the root since that night.

GleaningHEAVEN

Gleaning

The lesser harvest taken from the corners of the field every year since P+7, at 03:12 on the anniversary of the Harvest. Heaven names it for the law of the corners and the gleanings, which forbids the owner of a field to reap them and leaves them “for the poor and stranger” (Leviticus 19:9-10), and the Gleaning is the owner’s. Heaven left the field three years after the Harvest and took the first Gleaning in the fourth, by the law in the same chapter on the fruit of a newly planted tree, which says “in the fourth year all the fruit thereof shall be holy” (Leviticus 19:23-24). It gathers in one night. At 03:12 the rain goes up, the Pillar comes down, a Trumpet sounds, and whoever has ripened since the last rises into the light.
