THE STONES // HELD BY THE CAINITES

Old Nod

SHOW ON THE MAP

The oldest ground in the city, above the old harbor on the west shore of the bay: narrow streets that follow no plan anyone has found, and magenta light. It was the harbor town before the charter, and on the night of the Parousia more of its windows burned on than in all the rest of the city together. Every line in it is keyed, and its windows burn all night. CAM 07 stood on a mast above one of its roofs.

The city calls it the Stones, after the black stones the crews found under the harbor when they dug the cable vaults. Within a year of the charter most of it belonged to the Cainites, who had kept the location of Nod for eighteen hundred years and came to live on top of it. Every cable laid into Nodus runs through the Stones.
