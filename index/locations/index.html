THE STONES // HELD BY THE CAINITES

Old Nod

SHOW ON THE MAP

The oldest ground in the city, above the old harbor on the west shore of the bay, with narrow streets that follow no plan anyone has found, and magenta light. The narrowest of them, in the west of the district, are called the Narrows. It was the harbor town before the charter, and on the night of the Parousia more of its windows burned on than in all the rest of the city together. Every line in it is keyed, and its windows burn all night. CAM 07 still stands on its mast above one of its roofs.

The city calls it the Stones, after the black stones the crews found under the harbor when they dug the cable vaults. Within a year of the charter most of it belonged to the Cainites, who had kept the location of Nod for eighteen hundred years and came to live on top of it. Every cable laid into Nodus runs through the Stones.

Maren Holt kept the clinic’s appointments for ten years on a screen. On the morning after the Parousia she came in at eight as she always had, found the screen dead, and wrote the day out on paper from memory. Twenty-six patients came, none of them for the appointment they had, to ask whether what was in them would still work. It did.

She has kept the book since, and she writes down whatever name a patient gives her. The woman who shared the desk with her took the Seal in the first year and keeps the book of the Mercy clinics now, in one of the halls of Zion Heights, in the same neat hand.

THE BOOKING HALL

The Charter Line’s booking hall, off the old central hall of Central Station, is a long room of ticket windows under frosted glass, where the Notary’s clerks have sat since the Concord. Anyone in Pan with a pact to make queues for them. A clerk reads the terms aloud twice through the glass to the parties at the window, the parties sign in blood, the human last, and the entry goes onto the Ledger. The windows keep their old enamel, SINGLE, RETURN and SEASON, and the queue is the longest in Pan.

Under the charter the city bought its passes there. Its machines died with the grid, and it stood empty until the Courts opened Pan.
