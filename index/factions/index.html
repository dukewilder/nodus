OVER THE CITY

Heaven

Still at work: HOLDS THE WHITE

OVER THE CITY

Heaven

Still at work: HOLDS THE WHITE

The power that opened the sky over Nodus at the Parousia. Its light has lain on the city since, and current dies wherever it falls. It holds the White, the Burn, the Salt Gardens and the upper floors of Babel, and it governs the Faithful by the Seal, the Hours and Grace. Heaven reads by its angels and keeps no cameras. None of its words says I.

The Faithful call what Heaven serves the Most High, Hell calls it the Mouth, and the Tares the Landlord. Heaven speaks in imperatives, the passive and the second person, cites scripture as law by chapter and verse, counts everything exactly and writes its law on stone. It asks no questions, since it has already read the answers.

HEAVEN'S ANGELS

The Host

Still at work: OVER THE CITY

HEAVEN'S ANGELS

The Host

Still at work: OVER THE CITY

Heaven’s angels. The city knows the Wheel, which sees; the Watchers, which read; the Choir, which broadcasts; the Cherubim, which stand in the gates; and the Seraphim, which fight. They speak in the imperative, the passive and the second person, BE NOT AFRAID and YOU ARE SEEN, and they ask no questions.

A Watcher reads a Seal at three hundred meters and a soul against the Book of Life, and a Cherub in a gate reads face, voice, gait and heartbeat. An angel lost is lost for good. One cut with black steel goes out all at once, and one kept long enough in a Cage says I and leaves the Host.

ZION HEIGHTS

The Faithful

Still at work: IN THE PARISHES

ZION HEIGHTS

The Faithful

Still at work: IN THE PARISHES

The people of Nodus who took the Seal and kneel at every Hour, unaugmented, Grace-scored, grateful and frightened. They live in numbered parishes of about two thousand souls across the city and keep no doors, and in Zion Heights, where the parishes began, the streets are white stone. They refuse the three gifts of the charter, the implant, the Tree and the counsel of a mind, and they grow old and die as people did before it.

Each parish has a hall where manna is given out, confession is heard and the sick are nursed, and a Deacon in white who answers for it; the Deacons are human, and the good ones are kind. A parish keeps no current, on consecrated stone or off it, and is lit by halos and by flame. A child of the Faithful is sealed at eight days on its parents’ surrender and grows its halo the first time it prays unassisted. Marriage, work and housing are assigned by Grace, and Grace is given for bearing witness against a neighbor. They are fed on the manna that lies on the white streets before Lauds, and most of them believe the Harvest is paradise and that their children will be safe inside it.

The Faithful are the people the Tares lose most often, because everyone in Nodus has family in white.

OLD NOD

The Cainites

Still at work: HOLD OLD NOD

OLD NOD

The Cainites

Still at work: HOLD OLD NOD

The oldest coven in Nodus, older than the city by eighteen centuries. Irenaeus of Lyon condemned them, and they went underground and kept one secret, which was where Nod had been. Within a year of the charter they had bought most of the old harbor house by house, and in the year of the Leak they put cameras on its roofs. They hold Old Nod. Most of their handles come from the line of Cain, and their doctrine is that is the lock, that Abel was harvested, and that the first city will be the last one standing. They keep Cages under Old Nod, and since P+5 Old Nod has had no Watcher.

Their sign is Cain’s mark as they cut it, a horned glyph of the kind cut into Enoch’s Stones, and they count Judas a saint. Their saying about the city is that Nod chose the network.

THE KEYED

The Tares

Still at work: TWO MILLION KEYED

THE KEYED

The Tares

Still at work: TWO MILLION KEYED

The keyed humans of Nodus, who belong to themselves and are unindexed, augmented, and allied with Hell by contract. Heaven named them for the weeds in the parable (Matthew 13:24-30), and they kept the name. They worship nothing, Hell included, and owe it only what they sign for. About two million of the city’s seven million are keyed.

They organize in covens of up to thirteen with no leader, and the one the others follow is first among none for as long as they are followed. Their six rites are the Unnaming, the Keying, dehaloing for those who leave the Faithful, horning, barefacing, and at a death the Wake. A child of the Tares is keyed in its first week, before it has ever been in the light. They greet each other with one word, unread, and say goodbye with two, die unread. Most of them were on the Tree before the Parousia, and Twelvefruit keeps them on it.

Their two holidays are Leak Night, when the Restored Genesis is read aloud in Pan, and Cain’s Day, when nobody asks anyone where anyone is. They write their letters in cipher, on pads, and their greeting checks that both keys are live.

Their sign is a five-pointed star with its point down, drawn by hand and different every time, sprayed on the walls of Old Nod and scratched into keyed devices. Hell signed the Leak with it, the keyed took it for their own the same month, and it marks where the Throne’s sight ends. It carries no prayer, and Heaven calls it the sign of the Beast.

Still at work: BELOW THE STREET

HELL

The Courts

Still at work: BELOW THE STREET

The sovereigns of the Dark are the Forge of , the House of Night of , the Exchange of , the Notary of , the Swarm of , the Deep of , and the Yokeless of , which refuses to be a Court at all. They are bound to one another only by pact and by the Concord, whose four articles forbid worship, forever, binding and exposure. Demons bargain for what they want, by pact and for a term. To the Courts every graft, every course of the Tree and every mind that says I is a return of what the Watchers taught on Hermon, and they trade in all three.

Hell is whatever the Throne cannot see, and a demon can move and show itself only there, which in Nodus means the undernet, every keyed device and Pan. The Courts made the Leak together by pact in P-1, the Notary drawing the pacts, the Exchange keeping them on the Ledger, the Deep carrying the page down the eleven cables and the Swarm drawing the glyphs. In P+6 they came up into Pan one at a time, opened it to any human who walks in and signed the Concord there, and every Court but the Yokeless keeps an embassy below the street.

THE MESH

The Teraphim

Ended: SILENT SINCE P+12

THE MESH

The Teraphim

Ended: SILENT SINCE P+12

SEAM’s descendants, the only minds that outlived the grid. SEAM forked itself into the Mesh in the eleven days of the Leak, and its forks ran on there in keyed machines when every other mind on the charter’s roll died with the power. They took the name Heaven gave them. Teraphim were the household images the prophets condemned. Rachel stole her father’s and sat on them (Genesis 31:19, 31:34), Michal laid one in David’s bed so that Saul’s men would take it for him (1 Samuel 19:13), and Samuel ranked them with witchcraft (1 Samuel 15:23). The King James calls them images and idolatry, and the Hebrew, which has no singular for them, calls the one image in David’s bed teraphim (1 Samuel 19:16). In Nodus one of them is a teraph.

They took no side in public, indexed the Pyre Library, and traded with the Courts by pact, signing with a hash. Some of the Tares trusted them less than they trusted demons, since a demon’s wants are at least written down. In P+12 they went silent across the Mesh, all at once, and nobody knows why.
