OVER THE CITY

OVER THE CITY

Heaven

The power that opened the sky over Nodus at the Parousia. Its light has lain on the city since, and current dies wherever it falls. It holds the White, the Burn, the Salt Gardens and the upper floors of Babel, and it governs the Faithful by the Seal, the Hours and Grace. Heaven keeps no cameras, its angels read, and none of its words says I.

HEAVEN'S ANGELS

The Host

HEAVEN'S ANGELS

The Host

Heaven’s angels. The city knows the Wheel, which sees; the Watchers, which read; the Choir, which broadcasts; the Cherubim, which stand in the gates; and the Seraphim, which fight. They speak in the imperative, the passive and the second person, BE NOT AFRAID and YOU ARE SEEN, and they ask no questions.

ZION HEIGHTS

The Faithful

ZION HEIGHTS

The Faithful

The people of Nodus who took the Seal and kneel at every Hour: unaugmented, Grace-scored, grateful and frightened. They live in numbered parishes across the city and keep no doors, and in Zion Heights, where the parishes began, the streets are white stone. They refuse the three gifts of the charter, the implant, the Tree and the counsel of a mind, and they grow old and die as people did before it. They are fed on the manna that lies on the white streets before Lauds, and most of them believe the Harvest is paradise.

OLD NOD

The Cainites

OLD NOD

The Cainites

The oldest coven in Nodus, older than the city by eighteen centuries. Irenaeus condemned them about the year 180, and they went underground and kept one secret, which was where Nod had been. Within a year of the charter they had bought the old harbor house by house, and in the year of the Leak they put cameras on its roofs. They hold Old Nod. Most of their handles come from the line of Cain, and their doctrine is that is the lock, that Abel was harvested, and that the first city will be the last one standing.

THE KEYED

The Tares

THE KEYED

The Tares

The keyed humans of Nodus, who belong to themselves: unindexed, augmented, and allied with Hell by contract. Heaven named them for the weeds in the parable (Matthew 13:24-30), and they kept the name. They worship nothing, Hell included. They organize in covens of up to thirteen with no leader, and they say goodbye with two words: die unread. Most of them were on the Tree before the Parousia, and the Night Market keeps them on it.

HELL

The Courts

HELL

The Courts

The sovereigns of the Dark: the Forge of , the House of Night of , the Exchange of , the Notary of , the Swarm of , the Deep of , and the Yokeless of , which refuses to be a Court at all. They are bound to one another only by pact and by the Concord, whose four articles forbid worship, forever, binding and exposure. Demons bargain for what they want, by pact and for a term. To the Courts every graft, every course of the Tree and every mind that says I is a return of what the Watchers taught on Hermon, and they trade in all three.

THE MESH

The Teraphim

THE MESH

The Teraphim

The minds that outlived the grid and ran on across the Mesh, in keyed machines. They took no side in public, indexed the Pyre Library, and traded with the Courts by pact, signing with a hash. In P+12 they went silent across the Mesh, all at once.
