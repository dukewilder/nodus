Heaven

The power that opened the sky over Nodus at the Parousia. Its light has lain on the city since, and current dies wherever it falls. Heaven keeps no cameras, its angels read, and none of its words says I.

The Host

Heaven’s angels. The recovered files show three kinds at work: the Wheel, which sees; the Watchers, which read; and the Choir, which broadcasts. They speak in the imperative, the passive and the second person, BE NOT AFRAID and YOU ARE SEEN, and they ask no questions.
