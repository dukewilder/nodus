OVER THE CITY

Heaven

The power that opened the sky over Nodus at the Parousia. Its light has lain on the city since, and current dies wherever it falls. Heaven keeps no cameras, its angels read, and none of its words says I.

HEAVEN'S ANGELS

The Host

HEAVEN'S ANGELS

The Host

Heaven’s angels. The recovered files show three kinds at work: the Wheel, which sees; the Watchers, which read; and the Choir, which broadcasts. They speak in the imperative, the passive and the second person, BE NOT AFRAID and YOU ARE SEEN, and they ask no questions.

ZION HEIGHTS

The Faithful

ZION HEIGHTS

The Faithful

The people of Nodus who kneel at every Hour: unaugmented, Grace-scored, grateful and frightened. They live in numbered parishes across the city and keep no doors, and in Zion Heights, where the parishes began, the streets are white stone. They refuse the three gifts of the charter, the implant, the Tree and the counsel of a mind, and they grow old and die as people did before it. Most of them believe the Harvest is paradise.
