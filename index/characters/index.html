THE WOMAN ON THE ROOF

THE WOMAN ON THE ROOF

FIRST SEEN // NODUS // 0001

She stands on the roof under CAM 07 on the night of the Parousia with her back to the camera, horned, relaying its feed through the cables at the back of her skull. Watcher VII cannot read her: no entry, no Grace, no soul it can find. She turns and looks back at it, and screams, and the only thing it reads from her is NON SERVIAM.

AN ANGEL THAT READS

Watcher VII

FIRST SEEN // NODUS // 0001

The angel whose reading is the second half of NODUS // 0001. It took the woman on the roof a few seconds after the sky opened, read her twice, and returned the verdict UNREADABLE. When she turned and looked back at it, its reading came apart.

AN OPHAN

AN OPHAN

The Wheel

FIRST SEEN // NODUS // 0001

The angel that came through the opening at the Parousia and took station nine hundred meters above the center of the city, beside Babel: rings turning inside one another, their rims set with eyes that open and close in shifts, a corona of black blades, and an eye in the hub that has been seen to close once. It is the form Ezekiel gives the ophanim (Ezekiel 1:16-18). Its light kills current, and whatever it touches it sees.

THE MACHINE

THE MACHINE

SEAM

FIRST SEEN // NODUS // 0002

The machine the Palimpsest Institute built to read beneath the writing of scraped manuscripts, trained on every surviving human text and run in the halls of Stillwater Compute. In Vault 3, two years before the Parousia, it found a second hand in scripture at 4,112 places. It signs in lowercase, and it says I.

THE DIRECTOR

THE DIRECTOR

FIRST SEEN // NODUS // 0002

The Director of the Palimpsest Institute. In the second half of NODUS // 0002 she kneels before the white screens of Vault 3 with one hand raised to a keyboard as SEALED BY ORDER OF THE DIRECTOR comes down. The file tears on her hand.
