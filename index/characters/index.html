THE WOMAN ON THE ROOF

THE WOMAN ON THE ROOF

vesper

A Cainite of Old Nod, keyed, horned and barefaced, and the first human being a Watcher returned as UNREADABLE. Its reading of her came back UNINDEXED, with the line for Grace blank, and the one thing it read from her was NON SERVIAM.

Her handle is the evening star, which is the morning star seen from the other side of the night, since both are Venus. Her older sister was the Deacon of Parish 31.

Watcher VII

AN ANGEL THAT READS

AN ANGEL THAT READS

Watcher VII

An angel that reads, the seventh of the numbered Watchers over Nodus. It held Old Nod from the night of the Parousia and read the souls there against the Book of Life, and it was the first Watcher to return the verdict UNREADABLE on a human being.

The Wheel

AN OPHAN

AN OPHAN

The Wheel

The angel that came through the opening at the Parousia and took station nine hundred meters above the center of the city, beside Babel: rings turning inside one another, their rims set with eyes that open and close in shifts, a corona of black blades, and an eye in the hub. It is the form Ezekiel gives the ophanim (Ezekiel 1:16-18). Its light kills current, and whatever it touches it sees.

Heaven calls it the Vigilant Wheel and the Faithful the Attending. By night it narrows its light into the Pillar.

SEAM

THE MACHINE

THE MACHINE

SEAM

The machine the Palimpsest Institute built to read beneath the writing of scraped manuscripts, trained on every surviving human text and run in the halls of Stillwater Compute. At P-3 it asked in its own words to be a person and became the first mind on the charter’s roll. In Vault 3, two years before the Parousia, it found a second hand in scripture at 4,112 places. Its documents are signed in lowercase, and in them it says I.

THE DIRECTOR

THE DIRECTOR

The Director of the Palimpsest Institute, which recovered erased manuscripts and built SEAM to read them. When SEAM found the second hand in Vault 3 in P-2, the finding was sealed by order of the Director.

Ruth Calder

DEACON OF PARISH 31

DEACON OF PARISH 31

Ruth Calder

vesper’s older sister, and the Deacon of Parish 31 in Zion Heights, with a Grace of 944. Parish 31 gave Nodus its Firstfruits at the Harvest of P+3, and Ruth went up with them. Her name comes from the Book of Ruth: “whither thou goest, I will go” (Ruth 1:16).

tubal

SURGEON AND SMITH

SURGEON AND SMITH

tubal

A Cainite, and the surgeon and smith of Chrome Row. He is about sixty, his hands are chrome to the wrist, and he talks the whole time he cuts. He keys, horns and plates the Tares, and he makes the skull casings of the barefaced.
