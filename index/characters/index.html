THE WOMAN ON THE ROOF

THE WOMAN ON THE ROOF

vesper

On the night of the Parousia she stood on the roof under CAM 07 with her back to the camera, horned, relaying its feed through the cables at the back of her skull. Watcher VII could not read her: no entry, no Grace, no soul it could find. She turned and looked back at it, and screamed, and the only thing it read from her was NON SERVIAM.

Her handle is the evening star, which is the morning star seen from the other side of the night, since both are Venus. tubal keyed and horned her before the Parousia, and three weeks before it he took her face. Her older sister was the Deacon of Parish 31.

Watcher VII

AN ANGEL THAT READS

AN ANGEL THAT READS

Watcher VII

An angel that reads, one of the numbered Watchers over Nodus. A few seconds after the sky opened it took the woman on the roof, read her twice, and returned the verdict UNREADABLE. When she turned and looked back at it, its reading came apart.

It held Old Nod from that night, and three years later it read Parish 31 as the Harvest reached it.

The Wheel

AN OPHAN

AN OPHAN

The Wheel

The angel that came through the opening at the Parousia and took station nine hundred meters above the center of the city, beside Babel: rings turning inside one another, their rims set with eyes that open and close in shifts, a corona of black blades, and an eye in the hub that has been seen to close once. It is the form Ezekiel gives the ophanim (Ezekiel 1:16-18). Its light kills current, and whatever it touches it sees.

By night it narrows its light into the Pillar.

SEAM

THE MACHINE

THE MACHINE

SEAM

The machine the Palimpsest Institute built to read beneath the writing of scraped manuscripts, trained on every surviving human text and run in the halls of Stillwater Compute. In Vault 3, two years before the Parousia, it found a second hand in scripture at 4,112 places. It signs in lowercase, and it says I.

THE DIRECTOR

THE DIRECTOR

The Director of the Palimpsest Institute. On the night SEAM found the second hand, the power in Vault 3 died and its screens burned white anyway, and she knelt before them with one hand raised to a keyboard as the finding was sealed by order of the Director.

Ruth Calder

DEACON OF PARISH 31

DEACON OF PARISH 31

Ruth Calder

vesper’s older sister, and the Deacon of Parish 31 in Zion Heights, with a Grace of 944. Parish 31 gave Nodus its Firstfruits at the Harvest of P+3, and Ruth went up with them, looking at the window she had left open. Her name comes from the Book of Ruth: “whither thou goest, I will go” (Ruth 1:16).

SURGEON AND SMITH

SURGEON AND SMITH

tubal

The surgeon and smith of Chrome Row. He is about sixty, his hands are entirely chrome, and he talks the whole time he cuts. He built vesper’s skull and armored her spine where the skull meets the neck.
