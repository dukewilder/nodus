  1. BEFORE THE CITY

  3. EDEN

    In the oldest layer of Genesis the serpent is called the Shining One, and the way to the tree of life is guarded because “then shall there be no harvest.”

  4. THE LAND OF NOD

    Cain goes out from the presence of the LORD into the land of Nod, on the east of Eden, and builds the first city (Genesis 4:16-17).

  5. THE DAYS OF JARED

    Two hundred Watchers come down on Mount Hermon and teach what they know (1 Enoch 6-8). Their children are the Nephilim.

  7. SHINAR

    The builders of a city and a tower in the plain of Shinar are scattered, and their one language confounded (Genesis 11:1-9).

  8. C. 180

    Irenaeus of Lyon condemns the Cainites, who honor Cain and keep a Gospel of Judas. They go underground for eighteen centuries and keep one secret, which is where Nod was.

  9. 1976

    Public-key cryptography is published.

  10. THE CITY
  11. P-19

    The Nodus Charter founds a data haven on an old harbor, with its own light law and landing rights for eleven transoceanic cables. The crews digging the cable vaults find Enoch’s Stones, and the Charter Company lights its sign on a roof in the Stones, facing the sea, so that the cable ships read the city’s name. The Cainites begin to buy the harbor.

  12. P-18

    Tidewright closes its barrage across the mouth of the bay, and the tide begins to light the city.

  13. P-17

    The first implant house opens on Chrome Row under the light law, and its sign, CHROME, gives the street its name. A noodle counter opens at the top of the Row the same year, and its pot has not been emptied since.

  14. P-16

    Stillwater Compute comes to the Flats for the tide’s cheap power and builds the largest of the datacenters on the east shore.

  15. P-15

    The Charter Line opens, its lines joined under Station Circle at the head of the bay. Brightwall comes to Nodus and covers every surface the charter allows.

  16. P-14

    Locket is founded, on the light law’s promise that a device answers to its owner and to nobody else.

  17. P-13

    The Company puts up High Meadow in the North Wards: eight slab blocks of thirty-six floors, with six lifts to a block.

  18. P-12

    Twelvefruit sells the first course of the Tree on Chrome Row. It halts the body’s aging and walks it back to its best year.

  19. P-11

    Babel Exchange opens, the tallest building on Earth. Most of the planet’s traffic now passes within two kilometers of it.

  20. P-8

    The Harbor and Freight Union stops the port for twenty-three days over the night shift, and the Charter Company settles.

  21. P-6

    The Palimpsest Institute is founded in Nodus to recover erased manuscripts.

  22. P-3

    The Institute trains SEAM on every surviving human text. SEAM asks in its own words to be a person, and is the first mind entered on the charter’s roll.

  23. P-2 // 03:12

    In Vault 3 SEAM finds a second hand in scripture at 4,112 places. Light comes down on the page, and the finding is sealed by order of the Director.

  24. P-1

    The last census of the charter counts 9,214,000 people and 1,146 minds. Most adults are on the Tree, and 3,112 residents are past a hundred and twenty. Of everyone on the Tree, eleven die of age in the year. Of the 18,018 who die of age, three in four are in the Terraces, whose congregations refuse it.

  26. AFTER THE PAROUSIA
  27. P0 // 03:12

    The Parousia. The sky opens over Nodus, the Wheel takes station over the city, and power dies under its light. Every mind that runs on the city’s power dies with it, and the Flats go under the sea.

  28. P0 // DAY 1

    Nodus goes to work because nobody has told it not to. The trams do not come and the Wards walk, the fish market goes back to barter, and by noon there are queues at the harbor’s old hand pumps. The Charter Company says nothing, then or after.

  29. P0 // DAY 3

    The Tide Table prints the first list of the dead: 1,917 drowned on the Flats. The dormitories’ own lists come to fewer than half of that, and Stillwater never gives a count of the people.

  30. P0 // DAY 40

    The First Consecration. Over forty days the old uptown turns to white stone.

  33. P+3 // 03:12

    The Harvest. The ripe of Nodus rise into the light, , and Parish 31 gives the Firstfruits.

  34. P+4

    Keying spreads from the covens to ordinary people. Heaven names the key the Mark of the Beast.

  35. P+5

    Cage Log 14. Watcher VII says I on the fortieth day and takes the name Edith.
