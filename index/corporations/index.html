The Nodus Charter Company

P-19 // CHARTER HOUSE, UPTOWN

P-19 // CHARTER HOUSE, UPTOWN

The Nodus Charter Company

The company that held the charter and ran the city from Charter House as a board runs a firm, so that for nineteen years Nodus had a chairman where other cities have a mayor. It built Babel Exchange, the Charter Line and the Charter Gardens, and it kept the peace with its own force, the Charter Wardens.

Charter House turned white in the forty days after the Parousia. The board knelt inside it on the fortieth day, and the charter was not spoken of again.

Babel Exchange

P-11 // THE HEAD OF THE BAY

P-11 // THE HEAD OF THE BAY

Babel Exchange

The tallest building on Earth, two hundred and twenty-six floors, where all eleven cables terminate in a vault six levels below the street. Traffic was sold on its exchange floors by the millisecond, and most of the planet’s passed within two kilometers of it. At the Parousia the light broke its crown and the top forty floors with it.

Since P+9 the front line of the war has run through its middle floors.

The Palimpsest Institute

P-6 // THE RIDGE, NOW THE SCRIPTORIUM

P-6 // THE RIDGE, NOW THE SCRIPTORIUM

The Palimpsest Institute

Founded to recover erased manuscripts, with a reading room under a dome and vaults beneath it. It trained SEAM at P-3, and at P-2 what SEAM found was sealed by order of its Director. Its ruins are called the Scriptorium.

In P+1 its stacks went onto the fire at the Burn, and the Faithful pulled the Institute down around its reading room. Pilgrims leave keys on its steps.

Tidewright

P-18 // THE BARRAGE

P-18 // THE BARRAGE

Tidewright

The company that laid the barrage across the mouth of the bay and ran its turbine houses. Twice a day the tide ran through them and lit the city. The turbines ran down at 03:12:46 on the night of the Parousia, and within the hour the Flats were under the sea.

The Tares rebuilt the tidal taps in the Drowned Quarter, and part of the city runs on them.

Stillwater Compute

P-16 // THE FLATS

P-16 // THE FLATS

Stillwater Compute

The largest of the datacenters on the east shore, which ran day and night on the tide. SEAM ran in its halls. They flooded on the night of the Parousia and were never pumped.

The Tares run cable through them anyway.

Brightwall

P-15 // EVERY SURFACE

P-15 // EVERY SURFACE

Brightwall

The screen company, which covered every surface the charter allowed, and the charter allowed nearly every surface. It wrapped the lower forty floors of Babel in screens, which the city called the face of Babel.

For eleven days in the year before the Parousia every one of its screens showed the Restored Genesis, and nothing Brightwall did would take it down.

The Charter Line

P-15 // CENTRAL STATION

P-15 // CENTRAL STATION

The Charter Line

The city’s transit, with its lines joined under Station Circle at the head of the bay, in Central Station. Its tunnels run under Old Nod, Chrome Row and the Flats, and its trams ran down the long roads of the Wards.

Its tunnels are Pan now, and its flooded Deep End station is where Radio Pandæmonium broadcasts.

The cable consortia

P-19 // THE LANDING

P-19 // THE LANDING

The cable consortia

Eleven transoceanic cables from four continents, laid and owned by ten consortia, each cable coming up the sand into a cable house of its own: TETHYS, HALCYON, MERIDIAN, KESTREL, ORPHEUS, PELAGIA, BOREAS, LODESTAR, SIRIUS, HESPER and PHOSPHOR. The last two were laid by one consortium and named for the evening star and the morning star, which are one planet.

At the Landing a light moves under the water along the cable trenches. It is ’s.

Stonecutter Holdings

P-18 // THE OLD HARBOR

P-18 // THE OLD HARBOR

Stonecutter Holdings

A company nobody could see into, which bought most of the old harbor house by house within a year of the charter.

It belonged to the Cainites, who had kept the location of Nod for eighteen hundred years and came to live on top of Enoch’s Stones as soon as the crews found them.

The clinics of Chrome Row

P-17 // CHROME ROW

P-17 // CHROME ROW

The clinics of Chrome Row

Under the light law any clinic could fit any implant into anyone who could pay, and the clinics set up along one avenue. The first implant house had one word on its sign, CHROME, and gave the street its name. The Row’s signs went dark at the Parousia and have not been lit since.

The clinics went under the street. One of them had been down there all along, and three weeks before the Parousia it took a woman’s face. The Forge keeps them now.

Twelvefruit

P-12 // CHROME ROW

P-12 // CHROME ROW

Twelvefruit

The firm that sold the Tree. Its first course was given on Chrome Row twelve years before the Parousia, dear at first and cheap within a few years, and by the last year of the charter most adults in the city were on it. The name is from the tree of life at the end of scripture, which “bare twelve manner of fruits, and yielded her fruit every month” (Revelation 22:2). Its founders chose it as a boast, as the Company had chosen Babel.

At P+2 the Mercy clinics began taking the Faithful off the Tree. The Night Market sells the course in Pan.

Locket

P-14 // THE FLATS

P-14 // THE FLATS

Locket

The company that made the phones of Nodus, on the light law’s promise that a device answers to its owner and to nobody else, and sold them to the rest of the world on the same promise. Its engineers lived on the Flats.

It died with the grid at 03:12:46, and nothing it made has been made since. Its name is the city’s word for a phone.

The Charter Wardens

P-19 // CHARTER HOUSE

P-19 // CHARTER HOUSE

The Charter Wardens

The Charter Company’s own force, which kept the peace in Nodus for nineteen years.

They knelt with the board on the fortieth day and became the Host’s human auxiliaries. They carry guns, which work on people.
