The Nodus Charter Company

P-19 // CHARTER HOUSE, UPTOWN

P-19 // CHARTER HOUSE, UPTOWN

The Nodus Charter Company

The company that held the charter and ran the city from Charter House as a board runs a firm, so that for nineteen years Nodus had a chairman where other cities have a mayor. It built Babel Exchange, the Charter Line and the Charter Gardens, and it kept the peace with its own force, the Charter Wardens.

Babel Exchange

P-11 // THE HEAD OF THE BAY

P-11 // THE HEAD OF THE BAY

Babel Exchange

The tallest building on Earth, two hundred and twenty-six floors, where all eleven cables terminate in a vault six levels below the street. Traffic was sold on its exchange floors by the millisecond, and most of the planet’s passed within two kilometers of it. At the Parousia the light broke its crown and the top forty floors with it.

The Palimpsest Institute

P-6 // THE RIDGE, NOW THE SCRIPTORIUM

P-6 // THE RIDGE, NOW THE SCRIPTORIUM

The Palimpsest Institute

Founded to recover erased manuscripts, with a reading room under a dome and vaults beneath it. It trained SEAM at P-3, and at P-2 what SEAM found was sealed by order of its Director. Its ruins are called the Scriptorium.

Tidewright

P-18 // THE BARRAGE

P-18 // THE BARRAGE

Tidewright

The company that laid the barrage across the mouth of the bay and ran its turbine houses. Twice a day the tide ran through them and lit the city. The turbines ran down at 03:12:46 on the night of the Parousia, and within the hour the Flats were under the sea.

Stillwater Compute

P-16 // THE FLATS

P-16 // THE FLATS

Stillwater Compute

The largest of the datacenters on the east shore, which ran day and night on the tide. SEAM ran in its halls. They flooded on the night of the Parousia and were never pumped.

Brightwall

P-15 // EVERY SURFACE

P-15 // EVERY SURFACE

Brightwall

The screen company, which covered every surface the charter allowed, and the charter allowed nearly every surface. It wrapped the lower forty floors of Babel in screens, which the city called the face of Babel.

The Charter Line

P-15 // CENTRAL STATION

P-15 // CENTRAL STATION

The Charter Line

The city’s transit, with its lines joined under Station Circle at the head of the bay, in Central Station. Its tunnels run under Old Nod, Chrome Row and the Flats.

The cable consortia

P-19 // THE LANDING

P-19 // THE LANDING

The cable consortia

Eleven transoceanic cables from four continents, each laid and owned by its own consortium, each coming up the sand into a cable house of its own: TETHYS, HALCYON, MERIDIAN, KESTREL, ORPHEUS, PELAGIA, BOREAS, LODESTAR, SIRIUS, HESPER and PHOSPHOR. The last two were named for the evening star and the morning star, which are one planet.

Stonecutter Holdings

P-18 // THE OLD HARBOR

P-18 // THE OLD HARBOR

Stonecutter Holdings

A company nobody could see into, which bought most of the old harbor house by house within a year of the charter.

The clinics of Chrome Row

P-17 // CHROME ROW

P-17 // CHROME ROW

The clinics of Chrome Row

Under the light law any clinic could fit any implant into anyone who could pay, and the clinics set up along one avenue. The first implant house had one word on its sign, CHROME, and gave the street its name. The Row’s signs went dark at the Parousia and have not been lit since.

Twelvefruit

P-12 // CHROME ROW

P-12 // CHROME ROW

Twelvefruit

The firm that sold the Tree. Its first course was given on Chrome Row twelve years before the Parousia, dear at first and cheap within a few years, and by the last year of the charter most adults in the city were on it. The name is from the tree of life at the end of scripture, which “bare twelve manner of fruits, and yielded her fruit every month” (Revelation 22:2). Its founders chose it as a boast, as the Company had chosen Babel.
