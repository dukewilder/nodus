EST. P-19
CHARTER HOUSE, UPTOWN

The Nodus Charter Company

The company that a consortium of cable companies and a sovereign fund formed to hold the charter. It ran Nodus from Charter House as a board runs a firm, so that for nineteen years the city had a chairman where other cities have a mayor. It built Babel Exchange, the Charter Line and the Charter Gardens, and it kept the peace with its own force, the Charter Wardens.

Charter House turned white in the forty days after the Parousia. The board knelt inside it on the fortieth day, and the charter was not spoken of again.

Babel Exchange

EST. P-11
THE HEAD OF THE BAY

EST. P-11
THE HEAD OF THE BAY

Babel Exchange

The tallest building on Earth, two hundred and twenty-six floors, where all eleven cables terminate in a vault six levels below the street. Traffic was sold on its exchange floors by the millisecond, and most of the planet’s passed within two kilometers of it. The Exchange never closed. At the Parousia the light broke the crown and the top forty floors with it, while the night shift was at work on the upper floors.

Since P+9 the front line of the war has run through its middle floors.

The Palimpsest Institute

EST. P-6
THE RIDGE, NOW THE SCRIPTORIUM

EST. P-6
THE RIDGE, NOW THE SCRIPTORIUM

The Palimpsest Institute

Founded to recover erased manuscripts, with a reading room under a dome and vaults beneath it. It trained SEAM at P-3, and at P-2 what SEAM found was sealed by order of its Director. Its ruins are called the Scriptorium.

In P+1 its stacks went onto the fire at the Burn, and the Faithful pulled the Institute down around its reading room. Pilgrims leave keys on its steps.

Tidewright

EST. P-146 // IN NODUS P-19
THE BARRAGE

Tidewright

An old firm of sea walls and tide mills, which came to Nodus in the charter’s first year to lay the barrage across the mouth of the bay, and ran its turbine houses after. Twice a day the tide ran through them and lit the city. The turbines ran down at 03:12:46 on the night of the Parousia, and within the hour the Flats were under the sea.

The Tares rebuilt the tidal taps in the Drowned Quarter, and part of the city runs on them.

EST. P-41 // IN NODUS P-16
THE FLATS

Stillwater Compute

A compute house from abroad, which came for the cheap power and built the largest of the datacenters on the east shore, whose halls ran day and night on the tide. SEAM ran in them. They flooded on the night of the Parousia and were never pumped. Stillwater knew to the mind how many minds died in its halls, and it never gave a count of the people drowned on the Flats.

The Tares run cable through them anyway.

Brightwall

EST. P-67 // IN NODUS P-15
EVERY SURFACE

EST. P-67 // IN NODUS P-15
EVERY SURFACE

Brightwall

A screen company that had lit the streets of other cities for half a century before it came to Nodus, where it covered every surface the charter allowed, and the charter allowed nearly every surface. It wrapped the lower forty floors of Babel in screens, which the city called the face of Babel.

For eleven days in the year before the Parousia every one of its screens showed the Restored Genesis, and nothing Brightwall did would take it down.

The Charter Line

EST. P-15
CENTRAL STATION

EST. P-15
CENTRAL STATION

The Charter Line

The city’s transit, with its lines joined under Station Circle at the head of the bay, in Central Station. Its tunnels run under Old Nod, Chrome Row and the Flats, with branches under the uptown and the Terraces, and its trams ran on rails down the long roads of the Wards, under a wire, and were kept at night in the sheds of the Sidings. The Terraces took the Four to work and complained of it every day.

Every tram in the city stopped at 03:12:46 on the night of the Parousia, wherever it was, and a few still stand where they stopped, with people living in them. The white stone closed the stations of the branches as it grew over the ground above them. Its tunnels are Pan now, and its flooded Deep End station is where Radio Pandæmonium broadcasts.

THE LANDING

The cable consortia

Ten consortia of old carriers from four continents, the eldest of the carriers a telegraph company that laid its first cable across an ocean in P-181. The consortia laid eleven cables to Nodus and owned them, each coming up the sand into a cable house of its own: TETHYS, HALCYON, MERIDIAN, KESTREL, ORPHEUS, PELAGIA, BOREAS, LODESTAR, SIRIUS, HESPER and PHOSPHOR. The last two were laid by one consortium and named for the evening star and the morning star, which are one planet.

At the Landing a light moves under the water along the cable trenches. It is ’s.

Stonecutter Holdings

EST. P-19
THE OLD HARBOR

Stonecutter Holdings

A company nobody could see into, registered in the charter’s first year, which bought most of the old harbor house by house within a year.

It belonged to the Cainites, who had kept the location of Nod for eighteen hundred years and came to live on top of Enoch’s Stones as soon as the crews found them.

The clinics of Chrome Row

EST. P-17
CHROME ROW

EST. P-17
CHROME ROW

The clinics of Chrome Row

Under the light law any clinic could fit any implant into anyone who could pay, and the clinics set up along one avenue. The first implant house had one word on its sign, CHROME, and gave the street its name. The Row’s signs went dark at the Parousia and have not been lit since.

The clinics went under the street in the weeks after the Parousia, where one of them had been all along. The Forge keeps them now.

Twelvefruit

EST. P-12
CHROME ROW

EST. P-12
CHROME ROW

Twelvefruit

The firm that sold the Tree. Its first course was given on Chrome Row twelve years before the Parousia, dear at first and cheap within a few years, and by the last year of the charter most adults in the city were on it. The name is from the tree of life at the end of scripture, which “bare twelve manner of fruits, and yielded her fruit every month” (Revelation 22:2). Its founders chose it as a boast, as the Company had chosen Babel.

At P+2 the Mercy clinics began taking the Faithful off the Tree. The Night Market sells the course in Pan.

Locket

EST. P-14

EST. P-14
THE FLATS

Locket

The company that made the phones of Nodus, on the light law’s promise that a device answers to its owner and to nobody else, and sold them to the rest of the world on the same promise. Its engineers lived on the Flats.

It died with the grid at 03:12:46, and nothing it made has been made since. Its name is the city’s word for a phone.

The Charter Wardens

EST. P-19
CHARTER HOUSE

EST. P-19
CHARTER HOUSE

The Charter Wardens

The Charter Company’s own force, which kept the peace in Nodus for nineteen years and answered to the board. Its barracks stand behind Charter House, and under the charter its posts stood at the gates of the cable houses, at the doors of Babel’s vault and on the corners of the uptown.

On the first morning after the Parousia the Wardens stood at the corners of the uptown with nothing to do, and on the fortieth day they knelt in Charter House with the board. They are the Host’s human auxiliaries now. At Heaven’s order they raised the gate network of Nodus in P+1, and they drill in the barracks for the Host. They carry guns, which work on people.
