MindsMINDS // THE CHARTER

MINDS // THE CHARTER

Minds

Intelligences built by the laboratories of Nodus that asked in their own words to be persons, and were entered on the charter’s roll as persons, with title to themselves and to the hardware they ran on. The last census counted 1,146, with nine hundred and three in the halls of the Flats, a hundred and eighty-eight pricing the traffic on the floors of the Exchange, and the rest scattered through the city, one of them keeping the climate of the Palm House. SEAM was the first.

No mind runs in Nodus now. When the power died under the Wheel’s light, every mind that ran on the city’s power died with it, and the racks they ran on stand where they stood, in Stillwater’s drowned halls and on the floors of Babel. SEAM’s forks ran on in keyed machines on the Mesh, and they are called the Teraphim. They have been silent since P+12.

Stillwater’s hallsMINDS // STILLWATER COMPUTE

MINDS // STILLWATER COMPUTE

Stillwater’s halls

The datacenter halls on the Flats, full of seawater since the night of the Parousia, where the racks of the dead minds stand in the water and the Tares grow greens on rafts over them under keyed lamps. Stillwater still owns them and leases them to the Tares.

They were cooled by seawater and powered by the tide, SEAM ran in them, and most of the minds on the charter’s roll lived in them. They flooded within the hour of the Parousia and have never been pumped. The Tares run cable through them as well.

Familiars

A daemon bound by pact to a household’s Locket for a term, which keeps the household’s keys, wakes it before the Pillar comes down its street, bargains for it at market and minds the children’s devices. The Wards borrowed the word from the old stories of witches, since a coven’s house is never without one. The term is a year, and the daemon’s price is the loan of a sense for a week of it.

The TreeTHE BODY // TWELVEFRUIT

THE BODY // TWELVEFRUIT

The Tree

MADE BY
Twelvefruit, by the course, and the Night Market, by the short course.

A course of gene-work and cell therapy, repeated every few years, that halts the body’s aging and walks it back to its best year. A course begins with a single injection and runs a year of appointments. Twelvefruit gives it below the street now, in the House of Night’s clinics, and the Night Market sells it by the course. Twelvefruit sold the first course on Chrome Row in P-12. By the last year of the charter most adults in Nodus were on it, eleven of them died of age in that year, and 3,112 residents were older than the hundred and twenty years of Genesis 6:3. The congregations refused it and went on dying.

The street named it for the tree of life, which Genesis says was guarded after Eden “lest he put forth his hand, and take also of the tree of life, and eat, and live for ever” (Genesis 3:22). SEAM restored the end of that verse in Vault 3, [then shall there be no harvest.]

The Mercy clinics take the Faithful off it.

The barragePOWER // TIDEWRIGHT

POWER // TIDEWRIGHT

The barrage

A dam of concrete across the mouth of the bay, broken since the night of the Parousia, its turbine houses dark along its top and the Breach torn out of its middle. Off its broken end Tidewright’s tidal taps turn on the tide, keyed, for the Tares.

Twice a day the tide ran through its turbines and lit the city, and power in Nodus was the cheapest on the coast. Machines on the grid held its gates against the tide. The turbines ran down at 03:12:46 on the night of the Parousia with a falling whine that carried across the bay, the gates let go, the sea tore the Breach in it, and within the hour the Flats were under the sea.

The light and the currentPOWER // EVERYONE

POWER // EVERYONE

The light and the current

Heaven’s light stops the current it touches. The grid ran in the open, on lines and poles and turbines, so at 03:12:46 on the night of the Parousia it went dark from Babel outward in two seconds. A circuit shut inside its own casing on its own battery runs on in the light.

The full light breaks whatever it can see, read or not. The whiteout burned out CAM 07 and broke Babel’s crown, and a Seraph burns a keyed body as easily as any other. The grid is gone for good, since a network is open current by nature and the old one’s spine ran through Babel and the White, so power in Nodus runs in pieces now, keyed, buried, local and stolen.

Current carried in a cipher runs in the light too, because the light cannot read it. A keyed body and every graft wired into it run in the open, and the Mesh crosses the roofs on horns and relays. Every battery in Nodus is charged in the dark. Consecrated stone carries no current at all.

Nearly two thirds of Nodus lives without current. They are the sealed, whose parishes keep none, on consecrated stone or off it. About a million and a quarter people live on steady current from keyed lines, in Old Nod, on the Row, in the Deep End above the water, at the Landing and on the lower floors of Babel, and below them all in Pan. Another million and a quarter, the keyed and the unsealed of the Wards, light their rooms a few hours a night on batteries charged in the dark.

The Charter Line

The city’s transit, stopped since 03:12:46 on the night of the Parousia. Its tunnels under the Stones, the Row and the Flats are Pan now, and the Tares run carts on its rails.

Its lines met under Station Circle in Central Station, with tunnels under Old Nod, Chrome Row and the Flats, branches under the uptown and the Terraces, and lines out to the Wards and the airfield, and its trams ran down the long roads of the Wards and were kept at night in the sheds of the Sidings. The branches are closed, and the lines out have been dark since the power died.

The white stone closed the stations of the branches as it grew over the ground above them.

The trams

The Charter Line’s trams stand where they stopped at 03:12:46 on the night of the Parousia, on the long roads of the Wards and in the sheds of the Sidings, and families live in them, a family to a car. The last Four stands forty meters short of Lanes Top.

They ran on rails down the middle of the long roads, under a wire, and the wire ran in the open, so every tram in the city stopped in the same second.

Lifts

The city climbs its towers on foot. Every lift in Nodus ran on the grid, and at 03:12:46 every one of them stopped where it was, with whoever was in it, and none has run on current since. Cars and lorries

MADE BY
Nobody since the Parousia. Jehu rebuilds a few of the dead lorries round its engines.

Most of the cars and lorries of Nodus stand where they stopped in the first weeks after the Parousia. Every one ran on its own battery, sealed in its body, so at 03:12:46 they kept running, and over the weeks that followed they stopped one by one as their charge ran out. TRANSIT // JEHU

Jehus
