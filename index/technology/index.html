MINDS // THE CHARTER

Minds

Intelligences built by the laboratories of Nodus that asked in their own words to be persons, and were entered on the charter’s roll as persons, with title to themselves and to the hardware they ran on. The last census counted 1,146. SEAM was the first.

When the power died under the Wheel’s light, every mind that ran on the city’s power died with it. Those that ran elsewhere are called the Teraphim.

MINDS // STILLWATER COMPUTE

Stillwater’s halls

The datacenter halls on the Flats, cooled by seawater and powered by the tide, where SEAM ran and where most of the minds on the charter’s roll lived. They flooded within the hour of the Parousia and have never been pumped.

The Tares run cable through them anyway.

THE BODY // TWELVEFRUIT

The Tree

A course of gene-work and cell therapy, repeated every few years, that halts the body’s aging and walks it back to its best year. Twelvefruit sold the first course on Chrome Row in P-12. By the last year of the charter most adults in Nodus were on it, eleven of them died of age in that year, and 3,112 residents were older than the hundred and twenty years of Genesis 6:3. The congregations refused it and went on dying.

The street named it for the tree of life, which Genesis says was guarded after Eden “lest he put forth his hand, and take also of the tree of life, and eat, and live for ever” (Genesis 3:22). SEAM restored the end of that verse in Vault 3: then shall there be no harvest.

The Mercy clinics take the Faithful off it.

The eleven cables

Eleven transoceanic cables from four continents, each a trunk of glass fiber as thick as a man’s leg, each coming up the sand at the Landing into a cable house of its own. Before the Parousia most of the planet’s traffic passed through them.

The single trench that carries all eleven cables from the cable houses at the Landing, under the old harbor and Chrome Row, to Babel’s vault.

It runs straight through Enoch’s Stones, and every cable laid afterward has followed it.

NETWORK // BABEL EXCHANGE

Babel’s vault

Six levels under the tower, where the cables end and open out into the racks that joined them to one another and to the world. Line engineers kept it running around the clock, and the light law forbade any of them to read what passed through it.

The Tares have held it since the war came into the tower.

LocketsSIGNAL // LOCKET

SIGNAL // LOCKET

Lockets

Phones made in Nodus under the light law, which answered to their owners and to nobody else, their maker included. Most of the city carried one, and the city called every phone a Locket.

At 03:12:46 the network under them died with the grid, and they ran on in people’s pockets on their own batteries with nothing to reach. Nothing has been made since. The Faithful carried theirs to the Burn. In Pan a Locket is an old case with a new battery, its back scratched with the Tares’ mark and a daemon in it.

Brightwall’s screens

Screens on every surface the charter allowed, and it allowed nearly every surface. The lower forty floors of Babel were wrapped in them, and the city called that the face of Babel. They have been dark since the Parousia.

For eleven days in the year before it they showed the Restored Genesis, and nothing Brightwall did would take it down.

The barragePOWER // TIDEWRIGHT

POWER // TIDEWRIGHT

The barrage

A dam of concrete across the mouth of the bay, with turbine houses along its top. Twice a day the tide ran through them and lit the city, and power in Nodus was the cheapest on the coast. The turbines ran down at 03:12:46 on the night of the Parousia, and within the hour the Flats were under the sea.

The light and the currentPOWER // EVERYONE

POWER // EVERYONE

The light and the current

Heaven’s light stops the current it touches. The grid ran in the open, on lines and poles and turbines, so at 03:12:46 it went dark from Babel outward in two seconds. A circuit shut inside its own casing on its own battery ran on, and CAM 07 kept watching the sky until the whiteout burned out its sensor.

Current carried in a cipher runs in the light too, because the light cannot read it. A keyed body and every graft wired into it run in the open, and the Mesh crosses the roofs on horns and relays. Every battery in Nodus is charged in the dark. Consecrated stone carries no current at all.

Generators

Flame

On the night of the Harvest a candle burned under the Pillar in the only open window on a street in Parish 31, and it was still burning when the light had gone. The Night Market sells the gas bottles, filled.

The Charter LineTRANSIT // THE CHARTER LINE

TRANSIT // THE CHARTER LINE

The Charter Line

The city’s transit. Its lines met under Station Circle in Central Station, with tunnels under Old Nod, Chrome Row and the Flats, branches under the uptown and the Terraces, and lines out to the Wards and the airfield, and its trams ran down the long roads of the Wards. The branches are closed, and the lines out have been dark since the power died.

The white stone closed the stations of the branches as it grew over the ground above them. Its tunnels under the Stones, the Row and the Flats are Pan now.

TRANSIT // THE CHARTER LINE

The trams

The Charter Line’s trams, which ran on rails down the middle of the long roads of the Wards, under a wire. The wire ran in the open, and every tram in the city stopped at 03:12:46 wherever it was. A few still stand where they stopped, with people living in them.

LiftsTRANSIT // EVERYONE

Lifts

Every lift in Nodus ran on the grid, and at 03:12:46 every one of them stopped where it was, with whoever was in it. Nobody has counted how many that was. The city has climbed its towers on foot since. The Babel Line is fought on the stairs.
