MindsMINDS // THE CHARTER

MINDS // THE CHARTER

Minds

Intelligences built by the laboratories of Nodus that asked in their own words to be persons, and were entered on the charter’s roll as persons, with title to themselves and to the hardware they ran on. The last census counted 1,146. SEAM was the first.

When the power died under the Wheel’s light, every mind that ran on the city’s power died with it.

Stillwater’s hallsMINDS // STILLWATER COMPUTE

MINDS // STILLWATER COMPUTE

Stillwater’s halls

The datacenter halls on the Flats, cooled by seawater and powered by the tide, where SEAM ran and where most of the minds on the charter’s roll lived. They flooded within the hour of the Parousia and have never been pumped.

The TreeTHE BODY // TWELVEFRUIT

THE BODY // TWELVEFRUIT

The Tree

A course of gene-work and cell therapy, repeated every few years, that halts the body’s aging and walks it back to its best year. Twelvefruit sold the first course on Chrome Row in P-12. By the last year of the charter most adults in Nodus were on it, eleven of them died of age in that year, and 3,112 residents were older than the hundred and twenty years of Genesis 6:3. The congregations refused it and went on dying.

The street named it for the tree of life, which Genesis says was guarded after Eden “lest he put forth his hand, and take also of the tree of life, and eat, and live for ever” (Genesis 3:22). SEAM restored the end of that verse in Vault 3: then shall there be no harvest.

Lenses

Replacement eyes ground on Chrome Row, which see further and in more of the light than the eyes they replace. They glow when they run hot: dim magenta at rest, white at full burn.

The eleven cablesNETWORK // THE CABLE CONSORTIA

NETWORK // THE CABLE CONSORTIA

The eleven cables

Eleven transoceanic cables from four continents, each a trunk of glass fiber as thick as a man’s leg, each coming up the sand at the Landing into a cable house of its own. Before the Parousia most of the planet’s traffic passed through them.

The TrunkNETWORK // THE CHARTER

NETWORK // THE CHARTER

The Trunk

The single trench that carries all eleven cables from the cable houses at the Landing, under the old harbor and Chrome Row, to Babel’s vault.

Babel’s vaultNETWORK // BABEL EXCHANGE

NETWORK // BABEL EXCHANGE

Babel’s vault

Six levels under the tower, where the cables end and open out into the racks that joined them to one another and to the world. Line engineers kept it running around the clock, and the light law forbade any of them to read what passed through it.

The camera masts

A dozen cameras on masts above the roofs of Old Nod, pointed at the sky, each on its own battery. CAM 07 was the seventh, and CAM 06 stands at the far left of its first frames.

The decoderSIGNAL // OLD NOD

SIGNAL // OLD NOD

The decoder

A decoder wired into the camera masts that turned the Choir’s band into text in the corner of the picture. It printed the first words of the Parousia, BE NOT AFRAID, and then YOU ARE SEEN.

Brightwall’s screensSIGNAL // BRIGHTWALL

SIGNAL // BRIGHTWALL

Brightwall’s screens

Screens on every surface the charter allowed, and it allowed nearly every surface. The lower forty floors of Babel were wrapped in them, and the city called that the face of Babel. They have been dark since the Parousia.

NeonSIGNAL // EVERYONE

SIGNAL // EVERYONE

Neon

The signs of Chrome Row and Old Nod were neon, magenta for the most part, and they lit the underside of the cloud every night; the rain fell cyan through the cyan ones. They went out at the Parousia, and CLINIC and CHROME have not been lit since.

The beacon

The light at the top of Babel’s spire, lit to warn aircraft. It was the first light in the city to die at the Parousia.

POWER

The barragePOWER // TIDEWRIGHT

POWER // TIDEWRIGHT

The barrage

A dam of concrete across the mouth of the bay, with turbine houses along its top. Twice a day the tide ran through them and lit the city, and power in Nodus was the cheapest on the coast. The turbines ran down at 03:12:46 on the night of the Parousia, and their falling whine is on CAM 07’s recording.

GeneratorsPOWER // OLD NOD

POWER // OLD NOD

Generators

Diesel, batteries and current from places nobody asks about. Old Nod got some of its power back this way after the Parousia, and its windows were the only lights burning in the city that night.

TRANSIT // THE CHARTER LINE

The Charter Line

The city’s transit. Its lines met under Station Circle in Central Station, with tunnels under Old Nod, Chrome Row and the Flats, branches under the uptown and the Terraces, and lines out to the wards and the airfield. The branches are closed, and the lines out have been dark since the power died.
