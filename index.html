The second file to reach the mount is older than the first by two years to the minute. It dates itself P-2, at twelve minutes past three in the morning by the clocks of Nodus, and it runs for twenty-one seconds. I joined it from two cameras in Vault 3 of the Palimpsest Institute: BENCH V3, which hung over the imaging bench through which SEAM read, and CAM V3-2, a corridor camera a few meters away. The first half is SEAM finding a second hand in scripture. The second is the finding being sealed.
