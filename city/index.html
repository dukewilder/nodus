THE HOURS

Heaven keeps the Hours by Nodus time, and the whole city keeps them with it, whatever side it is on. The strip opens on the Hour your own clock is in.

THE PARISHES

The Charter Wardens stand before the barracks and hear the day’s orders in Tongues through their commander’s mouth. On the first of the month the eldest Deacon reads the Order’s questions in the Tabernacle.

THE PARISHES

On the Lord’s Day each parish kneels in its hall, hears the Directive in force and then its Grace in Tongues, soul by soul, through its Deacon in the Choir’s voice.

18:00 // THE HOURS

VESPERS

Dusk. The landings sit, and on Burn Day the Faithful carry to the fire.

THE PILLAR // IN THE WHEEL

THE PARISHES

On Burn Day the Faithful carry what they have surrendered to the Burn from Vespers, and the Kindlers work the edges of the pile in the dusk.

THE WARDS

A block’s quarrels are heard on the middle landing of its stairwell after Vespers. Children play Salt in the street, Nell Garvie climbs the harbor light, and the turners close their stalls.

PAN

The Psalters on the quays write out the week’s hymns for the unsealed, a battery a sheet, and the runners come up to the Undertow for the night’s jobs.

21:00 // THE HOURS

COMPLINE

The curfew falls. The Pillar comes down on the Burn, and Pan comes up the stairs.

THE PILLAR // ON THE STREETS

THE PARISHES

The Faithful kneel for the Hour and say the fourth, the ninety-first and the hundred and thirty-fourth psalms under their breath. Then the streets on the parishes’ rolls are Heaven’s ground until Lauds, and nobody walks them.

THE WARDS

The criers climb to the roofs with the week’s chart of the Pillar’s round and a whistle. Old Nod stands at its windows through the Hour, which it calls standing by night.

PAN

The Night Market opens under the Burn and the Cold Store opens its doors. Radio Pandæmonium comes on in the Low Tuning.
