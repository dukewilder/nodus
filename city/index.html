THREE LIVES

Nobody in Nodus is without someone on the other side of the Street Between. A life in Nod, from the first week to the last night, as each side lives it.

SEALEDKEYEDOF NEITHER MARK

BORN

The seven days

SEALEDSealed on its eighth day in a parish hall, at an Hour, on its parents’ surrender. Its halo comes with the first prayer it says alone.

KEYEDKeyed in its first week, before it has been in the light, in the House of Night’s birth clinics under Old Nod or by a steady hand in the Wards.

NEITHERBorn on a landing by candle, the birth chalked on the stairwell wall beside the battery debts, and left as it came.

NAMED

Names

SEALEDBy the name the Book of Life has, given at the sealing and at the gates.

KEYEDBy a handle of its own, written lowercase, entered on the Ledger at the head of its first pact and scratched on a white stone.

NEITHERBy its family’s name and its work, and a charterer by the date the charter entered it on the roll.

FED

Food and drink

SEALEDManna, thin white wafers gathered off consecrated stone before Lauds, as much as its Grace allows.

KEYEDA portion in Pan, fat and blood, a battery, eaten standing up.

NEITHERLeaven’s bread at half a battery a loaf, and what the bay and the rafts bring in.

WORKS

Work

SEALEDWhere its Grace assigns it, at Bethel Works, in the halls, at the gates, and paid in Grace. Batteries go to the hall as tithe.

KEYEDBy pact, at the benches of the Row, the forges, the stalls of the Night Market, or a runner’s job at the Undertow under a surety’s tenth.

NEITHERAt whatever pays, charging, carrying, baking or winding lifts, most of them at three trades at once and sleeping in shifts.

MARRIES

Pairing

SEALEDAs the Deacon assigns, between sealed souls, by Grace.

KEYEDFor a season, read twice at the SEASON window and renewed at RETURN, with a pair of pads cut together for the term.

NEITHERBy hanging a parish door on a room together, its chalked number becoming the household’s.

PAYS

Prices

SEALEDNothing in the parishes, which give by Grace. A sealed worker of the Wards paid in batteries carries them to the hall as tithe.

KEYEDIn silver, on the Ledger, a thirty to the bundle.

NEITHERIn loose batteries, a charged one to a silver.

DIES

The dead

SEALEDCarried in Bethel white on the parish handcart, the mourners kneeling on the road at every Hour they meet. The Deacon reads its last Grace at the grave.

KEYEDUnread, if it dies keyed. On the third night its crew calls the shade at the Wake, and it gives away its own things.

NEITHERSat up with until Lauds behind a shut door, the long night, unless a late key reached it in time.
