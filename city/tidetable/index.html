THE WEEK

  • Burn Day falls on the sixth day. The Faithful carry from Vespers, and the fire is lit under the Pillar when the Hum falls back from Compline.
  • The Rialto shows its ninth film of the twenty-three this week, on the generator, from Vespers.
  • The sorting office asks that letters into the parishes be left open at the counter, as the parishes take them.
  • The Liftmen’s car at the Rookery stops at the forty-fourth.

NOTICES

DOORS

Parish doors, hung on your frame, a thirty, the number on the back left as it came. Keyed latches ten silver. LINTEL, THE DOORYARD, IN THE STONES.

BREAD

Forty ovens, raised with yeast and baked on gas. Half a battery a loaf. LEAVEN, NINE ACRES AND THE LONG ROADS.

WATER

Rain off Westfield’s roofs, filtered and boiled. A quarter of a battery a jug. Bring the jug. CISTERN.

CANDLES

Tallow’s candles by the dozen at every bread queue. Stubs taken back.

A ROOM

A low floor in Turbine Row by floor-right, its door hung and its latch keyed. A thirty or more. Ask on the landing.

THE LIFT

Forty-one shafts wound in the Wards. A battery up and nothing down. THE LIFTMEN.

WANTED

A second man with a spade. Ask the sexton at the Acre, on the West Road.
